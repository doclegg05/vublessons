Start with your body and your screen. Sit so you can read without leaning in. The screen should be about an arm's length away, with the top near eye level. If the words are too small, Magnifier makes everything bigger. Press the Windows key and plus to zoom in, and the Windows key and Escape to close it. Browser zoom is different. It enlarges one website, and Control and zero puts it back. Change one thing at a time, notice what got easier, and put shared settings back before you leave. If alerts keep pulling you away, Do not disturb quiets them while you work.
