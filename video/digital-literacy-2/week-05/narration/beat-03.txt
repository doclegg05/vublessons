Some features help far more people than you might expect. Captions help in a noisy room, in a quiet library, and for anyone who hears less than they used to. Keyboard access helps someone who cannot use a mouse. Press Tab, and a visible outline moves from one control to the next. Color is another one. If a schedule shows full shifts only in dark shading, some people will miss it, and so will a black and white printer. Put a word beside the color.
