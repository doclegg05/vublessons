An AI coding agent is a program that reads the files of an app, plans a change, edits the code, and can run checks, all from a request written in plain English. That sounds like magic, but it is closer to hiring a fast helper who has never met you. It does exactly what it understood, which is not always what you meant. Today you will direct one. Our app is a small, fictional community resource finder. You will ask for one change, read what the agent changed, test it, and report a problem. Then we look at what it would take to turn this page into a real service.
