Imagine three people editing three separate handouts and calling each one final. It becomes difficult to know which instructions to use. Instead, agree where the common copy lives and what each person is responsible for. The owner coordinates the final decision, the writer drafts, and the reviewer suggests improvements. [short pause] Give each person access appropriate to that task, and verify that the link opens for them. Working together at the same time is synchronous collaboration. Leaving comments for later is asynchronous collaboration. Neither is automatically better. Choose the arrangement that fits people's schedules and the decision needed, then record the agreed outcome in the shared place.
