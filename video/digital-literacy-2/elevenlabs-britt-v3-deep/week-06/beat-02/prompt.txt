A website can present information for people to read. A web app responds to input, such as filtering our resource list. Software as a service usually provides an ongoing hosted service and may include accounts, stored data, subscriptions, and operational support. Our one-file practice app is not all of that. [short pause] It has no real account system, private database, or payment service. This distinction helps you ask for an achievable first version. A convincing sign-in screen does not prove secure authentication exists behind it. Start by describing the useful behavior you can actually test in the browser, then treat hosting, security, and service operations as separate decisions that require more work.
