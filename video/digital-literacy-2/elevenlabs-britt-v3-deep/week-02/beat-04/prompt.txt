Suppose you find a useful service today and need it again next month. A note saying good website will not be enough. Record the organization, the page title, the web address, the date you checked it, and the particular fact you plan to use. [short pause] A bookmark helps you return, but it does not freeze the page or prove that the information remains current. In your handout, distinguish a checked fact from your own interpretation. If the page does not answer a question, write needs confirmation rather than filling the gap with a guess. This is the digital equivalent of writing the source beside a number in a household notebook.
