# Design intent for this lesson video

Audience: older adult learners, many of them veterans, in a computer lab. Digital Literacy Level 2. Learners watch at the start of class with a printed worksheet.

Planned silences (not dead air):
- After each "Pause the video now" line, a full-screen pause card holds about 10.5 seconds with no narration. It says "Pause, try this", gives the task, and names where it is on the worksheet. Learners pause the video and work.
- A topic divider card opens each new topic and holds about 3.45 seconds of silence before the narration starts.
- The last chapter ends on a short closing hold.
- The narrator (text-to-speech) pauses briefly between many sentences on purpose.

Everything else is fair game: a screen that does not match what the narrator says at that moment, text that is cut off, overlapping or too small to read, a misread or mispronounced word, an audio glitch or clipped word, a silence that is not one of the planned ones above, or a pause card whose task does not match the narration before it.
