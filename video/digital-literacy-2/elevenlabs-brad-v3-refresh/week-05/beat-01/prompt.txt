[warmly] Think about how you handle an unexpected request in everyday life. You might ask who is asking, why they need something, and how you can check the story. [encouraging] Those habits are valuable online too. In this lesson, we will examine a fictional urgent message, compare different protections for a file, and choose permissions that fit a task. [reassuring] You do not need to identify every kind of attack by name. You need a repeatable decision process: pause, inspect the evidence, verify independently, and choose an action that limits risk. Use fictional examples throughout. There is no reason to open a suspicious link or expose a private account to complete the practice.
