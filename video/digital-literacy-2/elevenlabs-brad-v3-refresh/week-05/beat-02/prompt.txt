[calmly] Start with your body and your screen. Sit so you can read without leaning in. The screen should be about an arm's length away, with the top near eye level. [short pause] If the words are too small, Magnifier makes everything bigger. Press the Windows key and plus to zoom in, and the Windows key and Escape to close it. [short pause] Browser zoom is different. It enlarges one website, and Control and zero puts it back. [short pause] Change one thing at a time, notice what got easier, and put shared settings back before you leave. If alerts keep pulling you away, Do not disturb quiets them while you work.
