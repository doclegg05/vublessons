[warmly] Picture a radio connected to headphones. Turning up the radio does not make the room speaker play if the sound is still going to the headphones. [interested] A computer can work the same way. First check whether the sound is muted. Then inspect the selected output device: speakers, a monitor, or a headset. Choose the device you intend to hear, begin at a comfortable volume, and play a short test. If that test works but a meeting does not, check the meeting app's own audio choice next. [encouraging] Change one setting at a time. This gives you evidence about what fixed the problem instead of leaving you with several unexplained changes.
