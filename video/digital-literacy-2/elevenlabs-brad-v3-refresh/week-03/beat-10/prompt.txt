[encouraging] [short pause] Pause and ask a partner to use your resource pack without your explanation first. [curious] Can they identify the next action on the handout? Can they find the total and explain what changed when one cost changed? Can they tell what each slide contributes? Record one point of confusion and revise it. Then reopen the export and repeat the affected check. [pleased] This is useful evidence of learning: the document has structure, the calculation responds correctly, and another reader can act on the result. Carry the same routine into a household budget, a community notice, or another task you choose. Create for a reader, test the result, and keep a recoverable original.
