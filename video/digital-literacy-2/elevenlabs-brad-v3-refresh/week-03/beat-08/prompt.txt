[interested] A useful instructional clip has a clear beginning, an understandable action, and a visible result. Trimming removes unwanted material at the start or end. Splitting allows you to work with sections inside a clip, such as removing a long unrelated interruption. [curious] Watch both sides of an edit: did it cut off the first syllable or remove the step that explains the result? Keep speech intelligible and check captions against the final audio after editing. Captions from an earlier version may drift. You do not need elaborate effects. [encouraging] The goal is for another person to follow the action, pause when needed, and understand what changed.
