A workbook can contain several worksheets. Within a sheet, columns usually have letters, rows have numbers, and a cell address combines both. B2 means column B, row two. Our fictional supply list puts item names in column A and dollar amounts in column B: paper at twelve, folders at eight, and pens at five. Include a heading that explains the units, so twelve is clearly dollars rather than boxes. Enter the values in separate cells instead of putting the whole list into one cell. Before calculating anything, check that each amount sits beside the intended item. A correct formula cannot rescue numbers assigned to the wrong rows.
