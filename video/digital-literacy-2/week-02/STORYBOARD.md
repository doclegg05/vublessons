- voiceover: "Sync is convenient because a change can travel between a device and an online service. The same convenience can carry a deletion. Watch the practice file disappear from one location and then the other. That is why sync alone is not a separate backup. A backup provides another recovery copy under an appropriate plan. Do not test deletion with your only important file. Use the fictional copy and explain which protection would help if the mistake spread through every synced location."
