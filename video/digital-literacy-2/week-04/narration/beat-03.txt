Our subject line is Review the computer-help handout. The message says which section needs attention, what kind of feedback would help, and when a response is useful. For example: please comment on whether the contact section is clear before our next practice session. That is more actionable than please look at this. Use respectful, direct language and explain abbreviations the reader may not know. Attach or link the intended file, then check that it is the correct version. Read the message once from the recipient's point of view. Could they act without asking what you meant? In this course, draft the message with fictional information; you do not need to send it.
