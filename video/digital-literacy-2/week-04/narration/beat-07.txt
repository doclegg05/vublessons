Before a meeting, test your headset. In Windows Settings, under Sound, choose the headset as the input and speak during the test. In the meeting, stay muted unless you are speaking, and raise your hand when the host asks for it. Turn on captions if they help. A webinar is different. The host controls who can speak, so questions go in the Q and A box or the chat. Ask before you record anyone, especially a group sharing personal stories. If your sound fails partway through, use the backup the host gave you, such as the chat or the shared notes.
