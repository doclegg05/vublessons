[warmly] Think about a time you needed to read a letter, hear instructions, or remember an appointment. You already know how to judge whether those everyday tools help you. [enthusiastically] We will apply that judgment to a computer. Choose one task that matters to you: reading a library page, joining a practice call, or keeping a household reminder. You do not need to know every setting. [encouraging] Our goal is to choose one useful change, observe the result, and know how to undo it. Keep a scrap of paper nearby to record the original setting. That makes experimenting safer, especially on a shared lab computer.
