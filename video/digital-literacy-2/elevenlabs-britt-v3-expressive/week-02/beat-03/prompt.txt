[curious] A page can be accurate and still be wrong for your decision. An official library page from another town may describe a real service, but not the one available to your neighbor. Ask who published the information, what evidence supports it, when it was updated, why it was written, and whether it answers your question. For opening hours or eligibility, go to the organization responsible for that service and check the details there. Search-result snippets may be incomplete. [interested] Compare two pages that disagree. [confident] Do not count votes between websites; identify which source is in a position to know, and confirm uncertain details through an independently found contact method.
