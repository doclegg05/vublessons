WV Veterans Upward Bound · IC3 Digital Literacy GS6 Level 2 + Web App Building

Mondays, September 28–November 2, 2026 · 4:30–6:30 p.m. Eastern Time
Six meetings · 12 contact hours · Computer lab instruction

For veterans who can use a mouse and keyboard, open a browser and manage basic files. Level 1 or equivalent experience is recommended. Ask the instructor for a refresher when needed.

What you will be able to do

  • Adjust device and browser settings for a task.
  • Create a practice appointment and choose what others can see.
  • Select a connection and a printer; verify the result.
  • Refine a search and justify a source choice.
  • Complete a fictional online form and check its confirmation.
  • Organize files, set access and explain a recovery path.
  • Structure a document for reading and revision.
  • Use a formula and format a small workbook.
  • Adapt a presentation and media for an audience and credit reused work.
  • Choose a communication channel and write a clear email.
  • Collaborate using comments, coauthoring and version history.
  • Explain online purchases, subscriptions and digital identities.
  • Explain device, file and account protections.
  • Recognize manipulation and choose a safe next step.
  • Demonstrate core tasks and complete the parallel post-test.
  • Describe a small app and write a bounded AI prompt.
  • Explain HTML, CSS, JavaScript and browser storage.
  • Test a prototype, revise one behavior and explain what a hosted service still needs.

How we will learn

Each meeting combines demonstration, a short captioned explainer, guided practice, a break, partner work and a check for understanding. Use fictional examples throughout. The final week supports either an approved AI tool or a supplied starter app with no AI account required.

Learning checks

The pre-test occurs before instruction in week 1. The parallel post-test follows the GS6 lessons in week 5. Each has 28 questions, four per domain, and produces printable graded results. The week 6 prototype uses a separate 8-point rubric. Scores guide practice; they are not certification results.

Materials and access

Bring your willingness to practice. A workstation, browser, headphones, word processor, spreadsheet app and plain-text editor support the activities. Captions, transcripts, keyboard navigation, text sizing and print copies are available. No real purchases, private records or paid AI account are needed.

Between meetings

Optional: repeat one class task with fictional data and note where you need help. Save your worksheets and assessment results before leaving a shared computer.
