Your six-week path

  1. Make technology work for you
  2. Find, judge, and organize information
  3. Create something people can use
  4. Communicate and collaborate with care
  5. Protect your work and show your skills
  6. Build a small web app with AI

Your course workspace

Each week includes a navigable lesson, captioned explainer, hands-on work and a printable worksheet. Your lesson position stays in this browser. Assessment answers are not sent to a server.
