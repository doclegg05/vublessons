Week 3WWorking in: Microsoft WordPart 1 · Why it matters

Bold is not a heading.

  1. 1A heading is the title of a section.
  2. 2Bold text only looks like a heading. Word cannot find it.
  3. 3A heading style, like Heading 1, tells Word: this is a title. Word then lists it in the Navigation Pane.

Bold is not a heading.

Ask: when you skim a long letter from the VA, what helps you find your place? Headings. Word can only list headings it knows about.

Week 3WWorking in: Microsoft WordPart 2 · Why it matters

Word document or PDF?

  1. 1Word document: for you. Keep changing it.
  2. 2PDF: for other people. It looks the same on any computer or printer, even without Word.
  3. 3Keep both. Change the Word document, then make a new PDF.

Word document or PDF?

Ask: who might get this handout? A neighbor with a phone, the library printer, a family member without Word. They all get the PDF.

Week 3XWorking in: Microsoft ExcelPart 3 · Why it matters

Every cell has an address.

  1. 1Columns have letters: A, B, C. Rows have numbers: 1, 2, 3.
  2. 2B5 means column B, row 5.
  3. 3A formula starts with =. Excel works out the answer, and works it out again when a number changes.

Every cell has an address.

Connect to a household budget: change one bill and the total should change too, without adding it up again by hand.

Crop the photo

Cropping trims the slide view only. The file in Downloads is unchanged; that is the post-test idea. If stuck: A small menu opened under Crop? Click Crop at the top of that menu. (No Picture Format tab? Click the photo once first.)
