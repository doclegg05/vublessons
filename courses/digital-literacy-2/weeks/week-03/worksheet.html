PART 1 OF 4 · MICROSOFT WORD

Make a handout with real headings

GOAL 1
POINT A · YOU START HERESeven plain lines of text, all the same size.
→
POINT B · YOU’RE DONE WHENA handout whose three headings show in the Navigation Pane, saved in Resource pack.
Know your screen: File: save your work. · Home tab: most buttons. · Numbering: makes 1, 2, 3. · Styles box: Heading 1, Heading 2. · View tab: Navigation Pane.
  1. 1
    Open Word. Press the Windows key on your keyboard. (Or click Start at the bottom left.) Type Word and press Enter.the Word start screen, with a Blank document tile.
  2. 2
    Start a blank document. Click Blank document.a white page with a blinking line at the top. The blinking line is the cursor: where your typing goes.
  3. 3
    Type the handout. Type the seven lines below. Press Enter at the end of each line.Computer help at the library
    Who it helps
    Anyone who wants to practice one computer task.
    Steps
    Choose one task.
    Visit the learning desk.
    Ask how to repeat it at home.    seven lines, all the same size and color.
  4. 4
    Make the title a Heading 1. Click anywhere in the first line, Computer help at the library. On the Home tab, in the Styles box, click Heading 1.the line gets bigger and turns dark blue.
  5. 5
    Make two section headings. Click in the line Who it helps. Click Heading 2. Click in the line Steps. Click Heading 2.both lines turn dark blue. They are a little smaller than the title.
  6. 6
    Select the three steps. Point just before the C in Choose one task. Hold the left mouse button down. Drag to the end of home. Let go.the three step lines shaded gray: selected.
  7. 7
    Number the steps. On the Home tab, click Numbering: the button with 1, 2, 3 on it.1., 2. and 3. in front of the three steps.
  8. 8
    Check your headings. Click the View tab. Click the box next to Navigation Pane so it has a check mark.a panel on the left that lists Computer help at the library, Who it helps and Steps.
  9. 9
    Save: open the Save As box. Click File at the top left. Click Save As, then click Browse.a box titled Save As.
  10. 10
    Make your Resource pack folder. On the left side of the box, click Documents. Click New folder near the top. Type Resource pack and press Enter.a new folder named Resource pack.
  11. 11
    Open the folder. Double-click Resource pack.the top of the box ends in Resource pack. The folder is empty.
  12. 12
    Name it and save. Click in the File name box. Press Ctrl and A together to highlight the old name. Type Computer help handout. Click Save.Computer help handout at the very top of the window.

IF YOU GET STUCK

A box asks you to sign in? Close it with the X. You do not need an account tonight.

Made a typing mistake? Press Backspace to erase the letter before the blinking line.

A heading is missing from the Navigation Pane? Click in that line, then click Heading 1 or Heading 2 on the Home tab.

PART 3 OF 4 · MICROSOFT EXCEL

Add costs with a total that updates

GOAL 3
POINT A · YOU START HEREThree supply costs on paper: Paper $12, Folders $8, Pens $5.
→
POINT B · YOU’RE DONE WHENA supply list whose total changes on its own when a cost changes, saved in Resource pack.
Know your screen: The formula bar: shows what is really in a cell. · A cell: the box where a column and a row meet. This one is A1. · File is at the top left, the same as in Word.
  1. 1
    Open Excel. Press the Windows key on your keyboard. (Or click Start at the bottom left.) Type Excel and press Enter.the Excel start screen, with a Blank workbook tile.
  2. 2
    Start a blank workbook. Click Blank workbook.a grid of empty boxes called cells. The cell A1 has a green border.
  3. 3
    Type the labels. Click cell A1. Type Item and press Tab. Type Cost in dollars and press Enter.Item in A1 and Cost in dollars in B1.
  4. 4
    Type the supplies. In A2 type Paper, press Tab, type 12, press Enter. Do the same for Folders and 8, then Pens and 5.Paper 12, Folders 8 and Pens 5 in rows 2, 3 and 4.
  5. 5
    Add the total. Click A5. Type Total and press Tab. In B5, type =SUM(B2:B4) and press Enter.B5 shows 25.
  6. 6
    Look at the formula. Click cell B5. Look at the long box above the column letters: the formula bar.the cell shows 25. The formula bar shows =SUM(B2:B4).
  7. 7
    Predict. Paper is going up from 12 to 15. Before you change anything, guess: what will B5 show? Say it out loud or write it down.your guess, ready to check.
  8. 8
    Change one cost. Click cell B2. Type 15 and press Enter.B5 changes to 28 by itself. Was your guess right?
  9. 9
    Save: open the Save As box. Click File at the top left. Click Save As, then click Browse.a box titled Save As.
  10. 10
    Open your Resource pack folder. On the left side of the box, click Documents. Double-click Resource pack.the top of the box ends in Resource pack.
  11. 11
    Name it and save. Click in the File name box. Press Ctrl and A together to highlight the old name. Type Supply list. Click Save.Supply list at the very top of the window.

IF YOU GET STUCK

B5 shows the letters you typed? It must start with =. Click B5 and type =SUM(B2:B4) again.

Typed in the wrong cell? Click that cell and press Delete, then type in the right cell.

Download the practice photo Its permission: you may use and crop it if you give this credit: Photo: VUB practice photo, used with permission.

Know your screen: File: where you save. · Insert tab: add a picture. · Design tab: choose a theme.
  1. 1
    Download the practice photo. Click Microsoft Edge on the taskbar. Click the Week 3 · Worksheet tab. Scroll to Part 4. Click Download the practice photo.practice-photo.jpg at the top right.
  2. 2
    Open PowerPoint. Press the Windows key on your keyboard. (Or click Start at the bottom left.) Type PowerPoint and press Enter.the PowerPoint start screen, with a Blank Presentation tile.
  3. 3
    Start a blank presentation. Click Blank Presentation.one white slide with two boxes: Click to add title and Click to add subtitle.
  4. 4
    Pick a theme. Click the Design tab. Click any theme you like in the Themes row.your slide changes color and style.
  5. 5
    Type the title. Click Click to add title. Type Need computer help?your title on the slide.
  6. 6
    Type the photo credit. Click Click to add subtitle. Type Photo: VUB practice photo, used with permission.the credit line under the title.
  7. 7
    Insert the photo. Click the Insert tab. Click Pictures, then click This Device.a box titled Insert Picture.
  8. 8
    Choose the photo. On the left side of the box, click Downloads. Click practice-photo, then click Insert.the photo on your slide. If a Designer panel opens on the right, click the X at the top of that panel.
  9. 9
    Crop the photo. Click the photo. Click the Picture Format tab, then Crop. Drag a black mark on one side inward. Press Esc.that edge trimmed away.
  10. 10

  11. 11

IF YOU GET STUCK
