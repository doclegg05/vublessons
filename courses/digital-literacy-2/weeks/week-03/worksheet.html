Build a clear resource handout, a small workbook, and a short presentation.

Name or learner code: ____________________

Use fictional information. Work with a partner, take turns, and record what you actually observed. Type below or print a blank copy. Typed worksheet responses stay on this page only; print before closing.

1. Identify the audience and action for a fictional computer-help handout.

2. Create a document with a title, two heading styles, a short paragraph, a numbered list and a meaningful link.

3. Use copy, paste and undo; record one place a shortcut helped.

4. Suggest or track one partner edit, then accept or reject it with a reason.

5. Open supplies.csv in a spreadsheet. Add =SUM(B2:B4); verify 25, then change paper to 15 and verify 28.

6. Build three slides: need, steps, help. Use one theme, readable type, useful image alt text and a data table or labeled chart.

7. Crop a copy of the supplied original image. Describe trimming, splitting and captions for a short video. Record any source and license.

8. Export the handout to PDF and reopen it. Record two checks, and explain when editable format or CSV would be better.
