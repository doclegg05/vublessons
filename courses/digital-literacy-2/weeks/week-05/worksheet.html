Words we use tonight

Tab
One of the page names along the top of Edge. Click a tab to switch pages.
Address bar
The long box at the top of Edge where you type a website name.
Cell
One box in an Excel sheet, where a column and a row meet.
Fill Color
The Excel button that paints the inside of a cell.
Chart
A picture of numbers, such as bars or lines.
Password
The secret word or words that open an account.
Passphrase
A long password made of several words in a row.
Two-step verification
A second lock. After your password, the site sends a code to your phone. Only your phone gets it.
Pressure words
Words that rush you, such as act now or stops today.
Link
Words you can click to go to another page. The words shown can differ from where the link really goes.
Trusted route
A way to reach a company that you already had before the message came: a letter, a card or a saved bookmark.
Report
Tell your email program that a message looks like a trick.
