Week 2 worksheet answer guide

Course home

Instructor guide. Accept equivalent evidence-based answers. These activities evaluate demonstrated skills, not speed.

1. Write a focused question about a local public service.

Question identifies a service and location.

2. Record an initial search and one refinement or filter. Explain its effect.

Refinement narrows the actual need without treating ranking as proof.

3. Record a source title, organization, URL, access date and useful detail.

All five source fields are recorded; details use no personal records.

4. Judge the source’s accuracy, perspective, bias, credibility and relevance. Name one check still needed.

Evidence-based judgments, including an uncertainty or direct verification step.

5. Complete the fictional practice form. Record a validation message and the confirmation.

Missing required choice is corrected; confirmation does not claim a real request was sent.

6. Create a folder and clear filename. Describe who controls a cloud copy.

Meaningful name and folder; account/service or organization controls access.

7. Choose permissions for a reader, a reviewer and a coauthor. Demonstrate one in the simulator.

Viewer, commenter when available, editor; named recipients and least useful access.

8. Explain when to use version history, a recycle bin or a backup. Explain why ZIP and read-only do not prove encryption.

History: earlier content; trash: deleted file; backup: separate recovery copy. ZIP bundles; read-only restricts editing.
