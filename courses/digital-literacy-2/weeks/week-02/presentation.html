Read-only is not encryption

Choose the protection you mean

Can read; cannot edit

Readable content · changes restricted

Read-only limits changes. It does not hide the content.

A key is needed to read

Protected content→key→readable content

Encryption protects readable content with a key.

Open or edit?

Password to open / password to edit

Inspect what the chosen password setting actually protects.

Illustrative example · select a choice to explore
Read the explanation

Read-only and editing restrictions limit changes. Encryption protects readable content with a key. Password-to-open and password-to-edit can protect different things.
