Week 1 lesson plan: Make technology work for you

Course home

Set up a comfortable workspace, organize a calendar, and solve everyday device problems.

Learning outcomes

  • Settings: at a lab workstation with Edge, each learner enlarges a webpage with browser zoom and returns it to 100% (Ctrl and 0), selects the headset as the sound output and confirms it with a test, and names which control (page zoom, display scale or brightness) fits a described reading problem. All three are correct with no more than one prompt, and lab settings are restored.
  • Calendar: using a calendar form (a demonstration account or the paper form from video chapter 7), each learner creates a fictional event with title, day, start and end, place and reminder, rereads it to confirm all five, and chooses free/busy sharing for a partner, explaining what the partner will see.
  • Connections and printing: given three jobs (external screen, wired network, flash drive), each learner matches HDMI, Ethernet and USB and finds one real port, then opens print preview for page 1 of the worksheet, confirms the printer name, and sets Pages to 1 and Copies to 1 without printing.
  • Automation: in Word, each learner triggers an AutoCorrect change (teh to the), undoes it with Ctrl and Z, and says in one sentence what a mail rule and a cloud service do.
  • Help: each learner writes a help request that names the app, the task, what they tried and the exact message, and records one specific piece of partner feedback.

Prepare the room

Each station: a headset, Microsoft Edge, Microsoft Word, and this lesson and worksheet. At the front: an HDMI, a USB and an Ethernet cable to hold up, and the projector showing Edge. The lab has no calendar account, so worksheet task 5 uses the paper form from video chapter 7 unless you set up a demonstration account. Decide before class whether one test print is allowed. Play video chapters 2 and 7 with captions to check sound. After your own run-through, select Start fresh on this computer at each station. Keep 3–5 printed pre-tests in case the network fails. No paid service, real purchase or new learner account is required.

For unavailable features, use the course simulation or role play and label the evidence as simulated.

Lab quick card: Windows 11

Click-by-click steps for this week’s tasks in Windows 11 with Microsoft Edge. If your screen looks different, ask your instructor.

To do thisSteps
Zoom a webpagePress Ctrl and + to zoom in, Ctrl and - to zoom out, and Ctrl and 0 to reset. Or open the browser menu (··· in Edge, ⋮ in Chrome) and use Zoom.
Make everything biggerStart › Settings › System › Display. Under Scale & layout, choose a size from Scale.
Make only text biggerStart › Settings › Accessibility › Text size. Drag the slider, then select Apply.
Choose the sound outputSelect the speaker icon on the taskbar, then the arrow next to the volume slider, and choose the headset. Or Start › Settings › System › Sound › Output.
Mute or unmuteSelect the speaker icon on the taskbar, then the speaker symbol at the left of the volume slider.
Screen brightnessDesktop monitor: use the buttons on the monitor. Laptop: select the sound or battery icon on the taskbar to open Quick Settings, then drag Brightness.
Easier-to-read colorsStart › Settings › Accessibility › Contrast themes. Choose a theme, then select Apply.
Browser downloads and site permissionsOpen Settings from the browser menu (··· in Edge, ⋮ in Chrome), then type downloads or permissions in the Search settings box.
Check a printer and its queueStart › Settings › Bluetooth & devices › Printers & scanners › Open print queue. Pick your printer from the Printers menu in that window.
Set a default printerIn Printers & scanners, turn off Let Windows manage my default printer, then select Set as default next to the printer’s name.
Print previewPress Ctrl and P. Check Printer, Pages, Copies and Layout before you select Print.
Share free/busy onlyOutlook.com: Calendar › Share › enter the person’s email › Can view when I’m busy. Google Calendar: Settings › Settings for my calendars › your calendar › Share with specific people or groups › See only free/busy (hide details).
Undo an autocorrect change in WordType teh and a space; Word changes it to the. Press Ctrl and Z right away to undo. Settings: File › Options › Proofing › AutoCorrect Options.

Using the chaptered video

Play each chapter inside its cycle, as the Show, from the video slide. Pause at the times named in the plan; the narration does not stop by itself. Ask learners to predict before a demonstration and explain the evidence afterward. Replay only the chapter needed for a refresher.

Facilitation and access

Demonstrate slowly, then let learners try. Offer keyboard and mouse paths, captions and the written transcript. Print large copies if needed. Every learner works at their own workstation; a partner coaches but does not take over. Provide extra practice time without requiring learners to disclose a disability.

Evidence to collect

  • Record one task you want to make easier and one skill you bring.
  • Increase webpage text with browser zoom (Ctrl and +). Record the control you used, then reset it with Ctrl and 0.
  • Select the headset as the sound output and play a test sound. Record where the brightness control is for your screen, and explain brightness versus contrast.
  • Match HDMI, Ethernet and USB to three jobs: an external screen, a wired network and a flash drive. Find one real port on your computer.
  • Create a fictional library appointment: title, day, start and end, place and a reminder. If no calendar app is set up, write the same five fields here on paper, as in video chapter 7, and mark it simulated. Reread it and check each field.
  • Choose a calendar view and a sharing level for a partner who only needs availability. Explain why.
  • Open print preview (Ctrl and P) for page 1 of this worksheet. Record the printer name, orientation and pages. Set Pages to 1 and Copies to 1. Do not print unless the instructor directs it.
  • In Word, type teh and a space; autocorrect changes it to the. Press Ctrl and Z to undo it. Record a useful help search and one piece of partner feedback.

Feedback and differentiation

Ask the learner to explain a choice and demonstrate the result. Mark Independent, With prompt, or Needs practice. Re-model only the missing step. For faster learners, ask them to test an edge case or explain an alternative; do not add unrelated tasks.
