Week 1 worksheet answer guide

Course home

Instructor guide. Accept equivalent evidence-based answers. These activities evaluate demonstrated skills, not speed.

1. Record one task you want to make easier and one skill you bring.

Accept a personally meaningful goal and existing skill.

2. Increase webpage text. Record the control used and how to reset it.

Browser zoom or site text-size control; demonstrate reset.

3. Select the correct audio output; explain brightness versus contrast.

Check output and mute; brightness is light output, contrast is light/dark separation.

4. Match HDMI, Ethernet and USB to three tasks. Check one actual device port.

HDMI: display; Ethernet: network; USB: suitable peripheral. USB-C capabilities require checking.

5. Create a fictional library appointment with a reminder. Record the fields you checked.

Title, day, start/end, location, reminder; time zone when relevant.

6. Choose a calendar view and a sharing level for a partner who only needs availability. Explain why.

Week for conflicts; free/busy for limited detail. Verify service-specific options.

7. Preview a one-page handout; record the printer, orientation and page range. Do not print unless the instructor directs it.

The selected printer matches the lab destination; one-page preview prevents waste.

8. Type a sentence with a name that autocorrect changes. Correct it. Record a useful help search and one piece of partner feedback.

Learner reviews the result rather than trusting suggestions; help search names the app and task.
