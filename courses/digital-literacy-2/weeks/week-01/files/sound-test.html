WEEK 1 · MISSION B · STEP 5

Headset sound test

Put your headset on. Click the button. You should hear three notes in your headset, not the speakers.

IF YOU GET STUCK

Heard it from the speakers? Click the speaker icon on the taskbar, click the arrow › beside the volume slider, and choose your headset. Then press the button again.

Heard nothing? Check that the headset is plugged in all the way and the volume slider is up.
