Lab quick card: Windows 10

Click-by-click steps for this week’s tasks in Windows 10 with Microsoft Edge. Windows 11 differences are in brackets. If your screen looks different, ask your instructor.

To do thisSteps
Zoom a webpagePress Ctrl and + to zoom in, Ctrl and - to zoom out, and Ctrl and 0 to reset. Or open the browser menu (··· in Edge, ⋮ in Chrome) and use Zoom.
Make everything biggerStart › Settings › System › Display. Under Scale and layout, choose a size from the Scale menu. (Same path in Windows 11.)
Make only text biggerStart › Settings › Ease of Access › Display › Make text bigger. Drag the slider, then select Apply. (Windows 11: Settings › Accessibility › Text size.)
Choose the sound outputSelect the speaker icon on the taskbar, then the device name above the volume slider, and choose the headset. Or Start › Settings › System › Sound › Choose your output device. (Windows 11: the ˄ arrow beside the slider.)
Mute or unmuteSelect the speaker icon on the taskbar, then the speaker symbol at the left of the volume slider.
Screen brightnessDesktop monitor: use the buttons on the monitor. Laptop: select the battery icon on the taskbar, then drag the brightness slider.
Easier-to-read colorsStart › Settings › Ease of Access › High contrast, turn it on, then choose a theme. (Windows 11: Settings › Accessibility › Contrast themes.)
Check a printer and its queueStart › Settings › Devices › Printers & scanners, select the printer, then Open queue. (Windows 11: Settings › Bluetooth & devices › Printers & scanners.)
Set a default printerIn Printers & scanners, turn off Let Windows manage my default printer, select the printer, then Manage › Set as default.
Print previewPress Ctrl and P. Check Printer, Pages, Copies and Layout before you select Print. Margins are under More settings.
Switch the calendar viewOutlook.com: select Day, Week or Month at the top right of the calendar. Google Calendar: the view menu at the top right (Day, Week, Month).
Set a repeat and a reminderOpen the event. Outlook.com: Repeat › Weekly; Remind me › 30 minutes. Google Calendar: Does not repeat › Weekly; Notification › 30 minutes. Save, then find the next occurrence.
Share free/busy onlyOutlook.com: Calendar › Share › enter the person’s email › Can view when I’m busy. Google Calendar: Settings › Settings for my calendars › your calendar › Share with specific people or groups › See only free/busy (hide details).
Undo an AutoCorrect change in WordType teh and a space; Word changes it to the. Press Ctrl and Z right away to undo. Settings: File › Options › Proofing › AutoCorrect Options.
