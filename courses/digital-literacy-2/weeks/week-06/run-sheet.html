Before class: print worksheet and answer guide; open the lesson and practice library; check projector, keyboard, sound and printer; provide fresh fictional files. Ask learners to open the worksheet, then play the opening video in the first 30 minutes. It stops at pause cards: pause at each one, let learners do the worksheet task the card names, then play on. Welcome, prior experience and mission overview share the next 5 minutes. No learner account or purchase is required.

Keys: arrows / Page Up / Page Down · F fullscreen · N notes · B blackout · number + Enter jumps. Use the mission chooser for A–D. The phase buttons jump to the first instance of that teaching phase.

Core route: four missions, individual evidence, transfer. Reference the answer guide for success criteria; the worksheet contains the click-by-click quick card.
