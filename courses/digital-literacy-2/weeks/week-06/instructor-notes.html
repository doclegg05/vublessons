Slide 87

Slide 90

Slide 93

Slide 97

You gave your section titles real heading styles in Word. What does the Navigation pane show?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: Every heading, so you can click one to jump to it. The Navigation pane is built from heading styles.

Slide 98

Why type =SUM(B2:B4) in Excel instead of typing the total yourself?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: The total changes by itself when a cost changes. A formula recalculates. A typed number stays the same.

Slide 99

Slide 100

How do you turn a Word handout into a PDF?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: File, Save As, then choose PDF as the type. Saving as a PDF keeps the layout the same on any computer.

Slide 101

Week 4: work together without confusion.

Two minutes. Ask what the class made in Week 4: a request with a deadline, a message with To and Bcc, a handout with a resolved comment, and four offer facts. Then the five questions.

Slide 102

Where should the deadline for a request appear in an email?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: In the subject line, and again in the message. The subject is read first, so the deadline belongs there.

Slide 103

You Bcc ten neighbors. One passes your message to a stranger. Did Bcc fail?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: No. Bcc never stops a message being passed on. Bcc only hides addresses from the other people who got the message.

Slide 104

Which greeting leaves nobody out of a message to a class of adults?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: Hi everyone,. Words that name everyone welcome everyone.

Slide 105

In Word, what does Resolve do to a comment thread?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: Closes it, and keeps it on record. Resolved comments are hidden from view, not deleted.

Slide 106

A page says "First month $1, then $15 every month." How much do you pay in the third month?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: $15. After the first month, the monthly price repeats until you cancel.

Slide 107

Week 5: protect your work, show your skills.

Two minutes. Ask what the class made in Week 5: a sign-in sheet that does not rely on color, a protected practice sign-up, and a checked message. Then the three questions.

Slide 108

In a four-color pie chart, what helps a reader who cannot tell the colors apart?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: Label each slice with its name. Words carry the meaning when color cannot.

Slide 109

On a practice sign-up page in Edge, what does two-step verification add?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: A second proof, such as a phone code. Someone with your password still needs the second proof.

Slide 110

A message claims to be from your electric company. Where do you find a safe number to call?

Ask everyone to choose before you select an answer. Correct answer: On your last paper bill. Use a number you already trust, not one the message gives you.
