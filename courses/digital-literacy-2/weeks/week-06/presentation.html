Week 6eWorking in: Microsoft EdgePart 2 · Why it matters

One change. Say what must stay the same.

  1. 1
    Ask for one change. A big wish like make it better cannot be tested.
  2. 2
    Say what must stay the same. Without that, the agent may change things you did not ask for.
  3. 3
    Say where it may work: change only this file.

One change. Say what must stay the same.

Ask: if you asked a contractor to fix one door, what would you say about the rest of the house? The same care applies to an AI agent.
