Week 4 · Answer and observation guide

Assess observable evidence. A spoken prompt is “With prompt”; using the quick card alone can be independent. Mark simulated work separately from hands-on performance. These observations do not change assessment scores.

Mission 4A · Send a clear request

  1. (a) A written message, such as email, with the details and a link to the shared handout, so the partner can answer later. (b) Tell the instructor in person now, because it is urgent and here. A phone call or text suits an urgent problem outside the room.
  2. To: Sam. Cc: Pat. A subject that names the task (for example, Please review the computer-help handout by Thursday); a greeting; one clear request; a reply-by time (for example, by Thursday at 3 p.m.); a courteous close. A partner reading it can say what to do and by when. The worksheet draft counts as a simulated email.
  3. To: Alex. Cc: Pat. Bcc: the 20 volunteers, so their addresses stay hidden. Pat’s Reply all goes to you and Alex only; Bcc recipients do not receive a Reply all. Schedule it for working hours, such as 9 a.m. on a weekday. Send it from the volunteer group’s account. Bcc does not encrypt the message or stop forwarding.
  4. For example: “Please turn on multifactor authentication (MFA), a second sign-in check such as a text code, by Friday. I am happy to help if you would like.” Both abbreviations are spelled out or removed, a clear time replaces ASAP, and nothing assumes age or ability.

Round 2 · Make it actionable

  1. Each rewrite names an action, the file and a time, for example: Please check the dates on the attached Saturday sign-up sheet and reply by Wednesday at noon. A partner can say what to do and by when.

Round 3 · Say it so anyone can follow

  1. Problems: BYOD, RSVP and EOD are unexplained abbreviations; two messages assume what someone can do or who is in their life. Rewrites spell out the request and offer help to everyone, for example: The meeting moved to Room B. Please bring your own laptop if you have one; we have spares.

Review: Reply all reaches the sender and the visible To and Cc names. Bcc names never receive it.

Mission 4B · Improve one shared copy

  1. Owner coordinates and sets access (editor); writer edits (editor); reviewer comments (commenter). One agreed copy, for example in OneDrive or Google Drive. Synchronous when everyone can work at the same time; asynchronous comments when schedules differ. A decision with a reason is enough; real sharing needs an account.
  2. The comment names a place, a change and a reason (for example, In step 2, add where the desk is, Room A on the first floor, so first-time visitors can find it). The writer replies, makes or declines the change with a reason, and resolves the comment only after that. The comment shows the station’s Word user name, not the reviewer’s.

Round 2 · Vague to useful

  1. Useful: the step 2 floor comment and the title comment. Vague: This is confusing, Fix it, Make it better, and I would never write it this way (it judges the writer). A rewrite names a place, a change and a reason, for example: In step 1, list two example tasks so a first-time visitor knows what to ask for.

Round 3 · Live or later?

  1. Tuesday at 10: together now. Night and day shifts: comments for later. Alex reads only: viewer. Pat approves: Pat is the owner with editor access; others comment. Write the final decision in the shared copy.

Review: A useful comment names the place and the change. Saying why helps the writer decide.

Mission 4C · Join with care

  1. The practice meeting is used correctly, and the microphone test shows sound if the headset has a microphone. Habits: test sound first, mute when not speaking, use raise hand, chat or Q&A as the host asks, turn on captions if helpful, ask before recording. In a webinar, attendees usually cannot unmute. Community check: hours, eligibility or cost, confirmed through the organization’s official website or phone number.

Round 2 · Hear and be heard

  1. The input meter moves while the learner speaks into the headset. The input device is set back afterwards. With no microphone, the learner names the steps and the evidence is recorded as simulated.

Round 3 · Meeting or webinar?

  1. Planning call: unmute or raise a hand; ask everyone. Webinar: Q&A or chat; the host. Support group: raise a hand or follow the host; ask the host and every member, and do not record personal stories without agreement. Training call: chat, as the host asked; the host.

Round 4 · Is the post right?

  1. Open Saturday 9 to 11: wrong; the page says Fridays 4 to 6 p.m. No ID needed: wrong; bring a photo ID or a piece of mail. Free coffee: not on the page. Next step: trust the pantry's page and phone to confirm.

Review: Ask before recording or sharing. Respect the group's rules and each person's choice.

Mission 4D · Check before paying

  1. A: $12 for 3 months (2 paid months after the free month) and $66 for 12 months (11 paid months). B: $60 whether you keep it 3 or 12 months, so B is cheaper only if you keep it a full year. Questions: when does the trial end and what will it cost then, how do I cancel, are there refunds, who is the seller? Payment: a credit card, or a digital wallet that uses one, because a wrong or unauthorized charge can be disputed. A person-to-person app is for people you know; money sent is hard to get back. No real purchase or payment details.

Round 2 · First-year cost

  1. A: $12 for 3 months (2 paid months) and $66 for 12 months (11 paid months). B: $60 for either. The free month runs October 19 to November 18, so cancel by November 17.

Round 3 · What is missing?

  1. Seller: StreamSmart Media LLC. Price now: free. Later: $6 a month. Renewal: automatic, monthly. Missing: how to cancel. Do not sign up until you find the cancellation steps; deleting the app would not cancel it.

Round 4 · Inspect a checkout

  1. Seller: Practice Learning Store. Total: $12, including $2 delivery. Terms: read before paying. Method: a credit card, or a digital wallet that uses one, because a wrong charge can be disputed.

Review: Deleting an app usually does not stop billing. Cancel through the provider and keep the confirmation.
