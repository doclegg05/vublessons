Week 4 · Lesson plan

Objectives

  • Choose a channel and an account that fit the message.
  • Write a clear email and choose who receives it.
  • Give, answer and resolve feedback on one shared copy.
  • Take part in an online meeting or community with care.
  • Check a purchase, subscription or payment before you agree.

Observable outcomes

  • Channel and account: given two fictional messages (a detailed request a partner can answer tomorrow and an urgent problem in the room), each learner chooses a suitable channel for each and explains why, and names the account a volunteer notice should come from. Both choices fit the urgency and the audience.
  • Email: in the worksheet, each learner writes a practice email with a specific subject, one clear request, a reply-by time and a courteous close; places three fictional recipients correctly in To, Cc and Bcc and says who a Reply all reaches; and rewrites a sentence to spell out abbreviations and remove an assumption about age. All parts are correct with no more than one prompt, and nothing is sent.
  • Feedback: in a practice handout in Word, each learner adds a comment to a partner’s handout that names the place, the change and the reason, then replies to the comment on their own handout, makes or declines the change with a reason, and resolves it. Each learner also chooses live coauthoring or later comments for a given schedule and names the access each role needs.
  • Meetings and communities: using the slide 14 practice meeting, each learner unmutes, raises and lowers a hand and mutes again, tests a headset microphone in Windows Settings when one is available, and lists four meeting or webinar habits, including asking before recording, plus one detail to confirm with an organization before trusting a community post.
  • Purchases and payments: given two fictional streaming offers, each learner works out what each costs for 12 months and for 3 months, writes two questions to ask before paying, and chooses a way to pay a new website with a reason, explaining why person-to-person apps are only for people they know. The arithmetic is correct and the reason matches the payment protections taught.

Teaching rhythm

Tell: explain the purpose using prior experience. Show: ask for a prediction and demonstrate one step at a time. Do: learners act at their own seats using the quick card. Review: explain choices and inspect evidence. Use N for slide-specific instructor notes and the answer guide for expected results.

Pacing and recovery

Protect the 8-minute break. Finish each essential Point B before optional extensions. Offer prepared examples, partner observation and paper annotation when tools are unavailable; record these as simulated work. Use the closing block for consolidation, not a new task.

Lab quick card: Windows 11

Check which account you are using
In Edge, select your profile picture at the top of the window. The menu shows the name and email of the account that is signed in. Select Manage profile settings to see more.
Add Cc or Bcc to an email
Outlook.com: in a new message, select Cc or Bcc on the right side of the To line. New Outlook app: Options › Show fields › Show Bcc. Gmail: in a new message, select Cc or Bcc on the To line.
Reply or Reply all
Reply goes only to the sender. Reply all goes to the sender and everyone on the To and Cc lines, never to Bcc. Check the list before you select Send.
Schedule an email
Outlook.com or new Outlook: select the arrow next to Send, then Schedule send, and pick a time. Gmail: select the arrow next to Send, then Schedule send. Check the time zone.
Add a comment in Word
Select the words, then Review › New Comment (or press Ctrl, Alt and M). Type your comment. If you see a Post button, select it (or press Ctrl and Enter).
Reply to and resolve a comment in Word
Select the comment and type in its Reply box, then post it. When the change is made, select More thread actions (···) › Resolve thread. If the comment shows a Resolve button instead, select it.
Share a Word file to write together (needs OneDrive)
Select Share at the top right, above the ribbon. Save the file to OneDrive if asked, enter the person’s email, choose whether they can edit or only view, then select Send. Lab computers have no OneDrive account.
See earlier versions of a Word file
Only for files saved in OneDrive or SharePoint. Microsoft 365: select the file name at the top of the window, then Version history. Word 2021 and earlier: File › History.
Test your headset microphone
Start › Settings › System › Sound. Under Input, choose the headset, then select the arrow at the right of its name. Select Start test and speak, then Stop test.
Mute and raise your hand in a meeting
Microsoft Teams: Ctrl, Shift and M mutes or unmutes; Ctrl, Shift and K raises or lowers your hand. Zoom: Alt and A mutes or unmutes; Alt and Y raises or lowers your hand.
Turn on meeting captions
Microsoft Teams: More (···) › Language and speech › Show live captions. Zoom: select Show captions on the toolbar, or More › Show captions. The host can turn captions off.
Cancel a subscription
Microsoft: in Edge go to account.microsoft.com/services, sign in, select Manage next to the subscription, then Cancel. Google Play: go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions, select Manage, then Cancel subscription. Deleting an app does not cancel it.

Week-specific preparation

Each station: Microsoft Edge, Microsoft Word, a headset (with a microphone if you have them), this lesson and worksheet, and scrap paper or the Windows Calculator for task 8. At the front: the projector showing Edge and Word, and a board for the warm-up answers. Learners have no email, meeting or cloud account, so the email in tasks 2 and 3 is a practice draft in the worksheet, the comments in task 6 happen in a local Word document by swapping seats, and slide 14 stands in for a real meeting; mark this evidence simulated. Show Cc, Bcc, Schedule send, sharing a Word file or version history live only from a demonstration account, never your personal inbox; otherwise use the practice views on slides 4, 5, 6, 11, 13 and 14. No purchase, payment detail or real store page is used. Before class, open a blank Word document and check that Review › New Comment, Reply and Resolve work. Play chapters 2, 4 and 6 with captions to check sound and rehearse the pauses at 1:29 and 2:54. Keep 3–5 printed worksheets in case the network fails. After your own run-through, close Word without saving and select Start fresh on this computer at each station.
