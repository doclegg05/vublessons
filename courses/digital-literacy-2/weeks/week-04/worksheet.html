PART 3 OF 4 · MICROSOFT WORD

Give feedback on one shared copy

GOAL 4
POINT A · YOU START HEREA short handout with no comments.
→
POINT B · YOU’RE DONE WHENA handout where the comment is answered, the number is added and the thread says Resolved, saved in Documents.
Know your screen: The Review tab: comments live here. · New Comment: adds a comment to the words you selected. · The comment appears in a box on the right of the page.
  1. 1
    Open Word. Press the Windows key on your keyboard. (Or click Start at the bottom left.) Type Word and press Enter.the Word start screen, with a Blank document tile.
  2. 2
    Start a blank document. Click Blank document.a white page with a blinking line at the top. The blinking line is the cursor: where your typing goes.
  3. 3
    Type the handout. Click the page. Type the four lines below. Press Enter at the end of each line.Computer help handout
    Step 1: Choose one task.
    Step 2: Visit the learning desk.
    Step 3: Ask how to repeat it at home.    four lines, all the same size.
  4. 4
    Be the reviewer: select a line. Point just before the S in Step 3: Ask how to repeat it at home. Hold the left mouse button down. Drag to the end of that line. Let go.the Step 3 line shaded gray: selected.
  5. 5
    Add a comment. Click the Review tab. Click New Comment.a comment box on the right side of the page, with a blinking line.
  6. 6
    Write a specific comment. Type Add the contact number after the steps so readers can find help. Click Post comment.your comment in a box on the right.
  7. 7
    Be the owner: make the change. Click at the end of the line Step 3: Ask how to repeat it at home. Press Enter. Type Help desk: 304-555-0142a new line under Step 3 that says Help desk: 304-555-0142.
  8. 8
    Reply to the comment. Click the Reply box under your comment. Type Added, thank you. Click the small arrow button.your reply under the comment.
  9. 9
    Resolve the comment. Click the three dots ··· at the top right of the comment. Click Resolve thread.the comment turns gray and says Resolved.
  10. 10
    Save: open the Save As box. Click File at the top left. Click Save As, then click Browse.a box titled Save As.
  11. 11
    Choose Documents. On the left side of the box, click Documents.the top of the box ends in Documents.
  12. 12
    Name it and save. Click in the File name box. Press Ctrl and A together to highlight the old name. Type Handout with comments. Click Save.Handout with comments at the very top of the window.
