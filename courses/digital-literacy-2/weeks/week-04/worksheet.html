Words we use tonight

Subject
The line people read first. It says what the message is about.
To
The person who needs to act on the message.
Cc
A visible copy for people who only need to know.
Bcc
A hidden copy. The other people cannot see these addresses.
Comment
A note beside a document that suggests a change.
Reply
Your answer, written under someone's comment.
Resolve
Close a comment once it is dealt with. It stays on record.
Free trial
Free for a short time. After that you may be charged.
Recurring charge
The same amount taken again and again, such as every month.
