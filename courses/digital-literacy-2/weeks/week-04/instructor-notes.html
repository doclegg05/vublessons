Week 4 · Instructor notes

Slide 1

Work together without confusion.

Welcome learners by name. This lesson uses fictional examples and existing tools. Open the worksheet before demonstrations. F toggles fullscreen; N shows notes. Arrow keys progress through shows before advancing slides.

Slide 2

Start with what you know.

8 minutes including the welcome. Invite a real example without asking for private account or benefits details. Connect existing experience to today’s task.

Slide 3

Your four missions.

5 minutes. Preview the task sequence and success evidence. Use prepared examples if software or accounts are unavailable. The break is protected; optional extensions can wait.

Mission 4A. App: Email simulation. Explain why this helps: The right request, account and audience. Connect to the learner’s own household or community task. Ask for a prediction before the show. Full procedural steps and expected evidence are in the worksheet and answer guide.

Ask everyone to choose before selecting an answer. Correct answer: Check and cancel through the provider. Deleting an app usually does not cancel billing. Follow the provider’s cancellation process and retain confirmation. Check the actual Point B evidence, not simply completion of a click. Slide 21

Show the evidence.

Ask each learner for observable evidence using the answer guide. Record independent, prompted or simulated work accurately. Optional extensions wait if core evidence is unfinished. Week 6 uses its separate 8-point extension rubric, not the IC3 test score.

Slide 22

Take one useful thing home.

Invite one specific transfer task, such as finding a service, saving a handout or checking a suspicious message. Do not require anyone to reveal personal information. Videos are optional replay resources; no additional viewing time is silently added to the 120-minute lesson.

Slide 23

Mission complete.

Collect evidence, not just self-reported confidence. Celebrate a concrete improvement. Close applications and restore shared settings. Keep support needs separate from assessment results.
