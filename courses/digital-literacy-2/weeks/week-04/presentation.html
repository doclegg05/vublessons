Week 4 · Communicate and collaborate with care

Week 4: Communicate and collaborate with care

Choose the right channel, give useful feedback, and make safe online choices.

  • Choose a communication channel and write a clear email.
  • Collaborate using comments, coauthoring and version history.
  • Explain online purchases, subscriptions and digital identities.

Choose a channel for the task

A quick status update, a detailed request and an urgent issue may need different channels. Consider the audience, sensitivity and response time.

Personal and professional identities

Use the account and tone appropriate to the role. Check the active account before posting. Separate credentials; use a trusted password manager and multifactor authentication where available.

Write an email someone can act on

Use a specific subject, a greeting, one clear request, needed context and a courteous close. Check the recipient and attachment before sending.

To, Cc, and Bcc

To names the main recipient. Cc keeps others visibly informed. Bcc hides that recipient list from other recipients; it does not encrypt the message.

Timing changes how a message lands

Check urgency and working hours. State when a response is needed. Scheduled send can help, but verify its settings and your wording first.

Inclusive language helps people respond

Use the name and form of address people prefer. Explain unfamiliar abbreviations. Avoid assumptions about age, culture, abilities or technology experience.

Participate in a community

Read the group’s guidelines, stay on topic and disagree with ideas respectfully. Ask permission before sharing someone else’s story or image.

Verify a community service

Use an organization’s official contact or website to confirm eligibility, availability and hours. A directory entry is a starting point; details can change.

Collaborate on one shared copy

Assign an owner, writer and reviewer. Agree on where the file lives and what finished means. Clear roles reduce conflicting edits.

Live editing or later feedback?

Synchronous work happens together. Asynchronous work happens at different times. Match the method to schedules and the decision needed.

Feedback names an improvement

Try: “The contact method is hard to find. Could we put it after the steps?” Be specific about the text, reason and possible change.

Acknowledge and resolve

Read a comment, ask if unclear, make or decline the change with a reason, then resolve it. Use version history if a useful edit is lost.

Meetings and webinars

Test sound. Mute when not speaking. Use raise-hand or chat as directed. Ask before recording. A webinar usually gives hosts more control than a small meeting.

Goods, services, and subscriptions

Goods are products; services provide work or access. Streaming delivers media over a connection. A subscription can renew until canceled.

Review a digital payment

A payment service sends an authorized payment request. Check seller, total, recurring terms, delivery and refund information. A familiar logo alone does not verify a seller.

Small in-app purchases add up

A download may be free while features cost money. Review purchase controls and receipts. Do not authorize a real purchase during this activity.
