Week 4MWorking in: Gmail (in Microsoft Edge)Part 2 · Why it matters

To, Cc and Bcc.

  1. 1
    To: the person who needs to act.
  2. 2
    Cc: a visible copy for people who only need to know.
  3. 3
    Bcc: hides each person's address from the others. It does not stop anyone from forwarding the message.

To, Cc and Bcc.

Ask: when is it a problem if 30 people can see each other's addresses? Bcc protects addresses. It does not make the message secret.

Week 4WWorking in: Microsoft WordPart 3 · Why it matters

One copy. Comments, not copies.

  1. 1
    Three people with three copies means nobody knows which is final. Keep one shared copy.
  2. 2
    A good comment says where, what and why.
  3. 3
    The owner makes the change, replies, then resolves the comment.

One copy. Comments, not copies.
