To, Cc, and Bcc

To names the person who should act. Cc sends a copy that everyone can see. Bcc hides those addresses from the others; it does not encrypt the message or stop forwarding. Reply all goes to the sender and everyone in To and Cc, so check who needs your answer.

Acknowledge and resolve

Read a comment, ask if unclear, make or decline the change with a reason, then resolve it. A resolved comment is hidden, not deleted. For a file saved online, such as in OneDrive, version history can bring back a lost edit.

Goods, services, and subscriptions

Goods are products; services provide work or access. Streaming plays video or music over the internet as you watch or listen: Netflix, YouTube and Spotify are examples, and hoopla is free with a West Virginia library card. A subscription can keep renewing until you cancel it.

Review a digital payment

Know how you pay. A credit card lets you dispute a wrong charge. A digital wallet, such as Apple Pay or Google Pay, pays with a card you saved. Money sent with Zelle, Venmo or Cash App is hard to get back, so pay only people you know. Check the seller, total and refund terms first.
