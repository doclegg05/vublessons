Assessment version 3 · Fictional practice scenarios

Name or learner code: ____________________

Read each situation and choose the one answer that best fits the task. Each correct answer earns one point. Total: 28. This is a classroom assessment, not a certification exam.

1. At a community computer lab, text is too small in the word processor, email app and desktop menus. Enlarging one webpage did not help those other areas. Which setting best matches the wider problem?

  1. ☐ Increase only the browser’s page zoom again.
  2. ☐ Change the default printer’s paper size.
  3. ☐ Increase the computer’s display scaling, then check the apps.

2. You are arranging two volunteer visits on different days next week. Both are saved in your calendar. Which view would best help you compare their times with your other appointments?

  1. ☐ A task list containing only tasks with no meeting times.
  2. ☐ Week view, which shows the days together for checking conflicts.
  3. ☐ Day view for today, without opening the days of the visits.

3. You create a repeating reminder for a Monday library visit. When you check next week, the reminder appears on Tuesday. What should you do before relying on it?

  1. ☐ Correct the repeat settings and check the next occurrence.
  2. ☐ Leave the rule alone because automatic reminders must be accurate.
  3. ☐ Keep adding separate Monday reminders without checking the original rule.

4. Your print preview for a community flyer shows the last line on a second page. You need each flyer to fit on one sheet. What should you do before making copies?

  1. ☐ Print only page one and let the missing line be left out.
  2. ☐ Increase the number of copies without changing the layout.
  3. ☐ Adjust the layout, check the preview again and inspect one test print.

5. You are setting up a separate account for volunteer work. You already have a personal account with a strong password. Which plan best keeps the two accounts protected?

  1. ☐ Give every volunteer the same password so the team can remember it.
  2. ☐ Use a different strong password and enable multifactor authentication where available.
  3. ☐ Reuse the personal password because it is already strong.

6. A teammate drafts this nonurgent message: “Look at the flyer soon.” You need their review before the community team meets on Friday. Which revision sets a clear response expectation?

  1. ☐ “Please check the flyer’s contact details and send corrections by Thursday afternoon.”
  2. ☐ “URGENT: look at this whenever you can.”
  3. ☐ “I will keep sending this until you answer.”

7. A volunteer guide begins, “Older people will need someone else to do this for them.” The class includes people with varied experience. Which replacement best supports everyone’s participation?

  1. ☐ “Everyone over a certain age should skip the practice.”
  2. ☐ “Anyone who needs help should watch instead of trying.”
  3. ☐ “Try these steps at your own pace, and ask for help where you need it.”

8. A library practice document will not save. You have tried Save As, but the app still displays “Folder unavailable.” Which request would give official support the most useful starting information?

  1. ☐ Send your account password instead of describing the problem.
  2. ☐ Name the app and version, the save task, what you tried and the exact error.
  3. ☐ Say only “My computer does not work” and wait for a guess.

9. Searching for “classes” returns cooking, exercise and computer lessons across the country. You need beginner computer instruction near Charleston, West Virginia. Which revision would make the results more useful?

  1. ☐ Search for “beginner computer classes Charleston WV.”
  2. ☐ Search for “classes” again without adding any details.
  3. ☐ Search for “popular activities” and rely on the first result.

10. A page selling a paid computer course says it is the only reliable way to learn. It provides no evidence for that claim. How should you judge the recommendation?

  1. ☐ Accept it because a business would not promote its own course unfairly.
  2. ☐ Assume that a polished layout proves the claim.
  3. ☐ Consider the seller’s interest and compare the claim with independent evidence.

11. You find an official local workshop announcement from three years ago. It accurately describes the event held then, but you need a session you can attend now. What still needs checking?

  1. ☐ Nothing; an accurate past announcement guarantees a current session.
  2. ☐ Whether the program still runs and what its current schedule is.
  3. ☐ Only whether the old announcement has a familiar logo.

12. After submitting the fictional computer-help form, you see a confirmation for “phone help,” although you intended an in-person visit. What does checking this confirmation tell you to do?

  1. ☐ Use the available correction or contact method to fix the requested help type.
  2. ☐ Assume the confirmation is unimportant because the form was submitted.
  3. ☐ Submit several more requests without checking whether they replace the first.

13. Your handout’s section titles look large and bold, but the document’s heading navigation does not list them. What is the most useful correction?

  1. ☐ Make the same ordinary paragraphs even larger.
  2. ☐ Add more blank lines before each title.
  3. ☐ Apply real heading styles to the section titles.

14. A practice budget shows three costs: 12, 8 and 5, with a total of 25. You want to check whether the total really uses all three cells. Which test gives useful evidence?

  1. ☐ Rename the worksheet and assume the total is now verified.
  2. ☐ Change 12 to 15 and check that the total updates to 28.
  3. ☐ Change the total cell’s color and check that it stays 25.

15. A practice image has written permission allowing reuse and cropping if the creator, title, source and license are credited. You want to crop it for a flyer. Which plan follows those stated conditions?

  1. ☐ Crop a copy and include the required credit with the reused image.
  2. ☐ Crop it and omit the credit because only part of the image remains.
  3. ☐ Keep only the creator’s name because the other required details are optional.

16. Your finished community handout is ready for neighbors to read and print. They do not need to edit it, and you want to preserve its page layout. Which delivery fits that purpose?

  1. ☐ Export CSV because it preserves document layout and images.
  2. ☐ Send a photo of the screen without checking whether all the text is readable.
  3. ☐ Export a PDF, then open it to check page breaks, links and missing content.

17. Your email to a community organizer already has a clear subject. The body only says, “Here is the flyer.” You need the organizer to check the room number before printing. What should you add?

  1. ☐ A second copy of the same attachment without explaining the task.
  2. ☐ A specific request to verify the room number, with the deadline and attached draft.
  3. ☐ More praise for the flyer without saying what needs checking.

18. You sent a volunteer announcement using Bcc. A partner says this also prevents recipients from forwarding its contents. Which statement correctly explains the limit?

  1. ☐ Bcc hides those recipient addresses; it does not stop forwarding the message.
  2. ☐ Bcc encrypts the message and makes forwarding impossible.
  3. ☐ Bcc removes the attachment as soon as a recipient opens it.

19. A fictional app trial says: “No charge for seven days; then $8 each month unless canceled.” You only need the app for one weekend. Which conclusion follows from these terms?

  1. ☐ Deleting its desktop shortcut ends the subscription automatically.
  2. ☐ The seven free days mean all later use will also be free.
  3. ☐ Keeping the trial active after seven days can start a recurring charge.

20. A fictional free game offers a $2 upgrade. Its purchase screen also offers a way to require approval for future purchases. Why is that control useful?

  1. ☐ It proves that no charge has ever been made in the app.
  2. ☐ It can help prevent additional purchases from being authorized without review.
  3. ☐ It makes every paid upgrade free from then on.

21. Two volunteers cannot work at the same time. One can draft a handout today, and the other can review it tomorrow. Which plan fits their schedules while keeping one shared copy?

  1. ☐ Leave comments in the shared draft for the other person to review later.
  2. ☐ Require both volunteers to type in the file during a live call today.
  3. ☐ Create separate copies without agreeing how changes will be combined.

22. A named reviewer has finished commenting on your draft. You now ask that person to make the agreed corrections directly, while everyone else should still only read it. What access change fits?

  1. ☐ Make anyone with the link an editor to avoid changing one person’s role.
  2. ☐ Keep the reviewer view-only and expect direct edits to work.
  3. ☐ Give that reviewer editing access and keep the others as viewers.

23. A partner comments, “Add the help desk’s contact method after step 3 so readers know where to ask questions.” You are responsible for revising the handout. What is a useful response?

  1. ☐ Change the background color and mark the contact issue resolved.
  2. ☐ Add the verified contact information and acknowledge how you addressed the comment.
  3. ☐ Delete the comment without checking the missing information.

24. During a live workshop call, background noise from your room makes it hard for others to hear the speaker. You are listening and do not need to speak yet. What should you do?

  1. ☐ Mute your microphone and unmute when it is your turn to speak.
  2. ☐ Raise your speaker volume to cover the noise.
  3. ☐ Start a recording so the background noise will stop.

25. A practice page marks completed steps only in green and unfinished steps only in red. Some learners cannot distinguish those colors. Which change makes the status clearer?

  1. ☐ Use brighter red and green but keep color as the only signal.
  2. ☐ Move the color key to the bottom without labeling the steps.
  3. ☐ Add visible “Complete” and “Not yet” labels beside the colored indicators.

26. Notifications from a community group keep pulling you away from your practice task. You feel you must check every alert so you do not miss out. Which response supports your own learning goal?

  1. ☐ Join more groups so you are less likely to miss an update.
  2. ☐ Adjust nonessential alerts and choose a planned time to check the group.
  3. ☐ Open every alert immediately, even when it interrupts the task.

27. A webpage that only displays a written community schedule asks to use your camera. Nothing in your task needs a photo or video call. What is the most appropriate choice?

  1. ☐ Deny camera access because the permission does not fit the task.
  2. ☐ Allow access because every permission request is required to read a page.
  3. ☐ Allow it because the page uses a familiar community logo.

28. Your practice laptop uses device encryption, but you leave it unlocked and signed in at a shared table. What protection is still needed when you step away?

  1. ☐ Nothing; encryption makes an unlocked account inaccessible to everyone else.
  2. ☐ Rename the files so the open account can safely remain unattended.
  3. ☐ Lock the session so another person cannot use the open account.
