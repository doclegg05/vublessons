Assessment version 2 · Fictional practice scenarios

Name or learner code: ____________________

Read each situation and choose the one answer that best fits the task. Each correct answer earns one point. Total: 28. This is a classroom assessment, not a certification exam.

1. You are reading a community workshop page at home. Its words are too small, but your email app and desktop labels are readable. What should you try first?

  1. ☐ Enlarge every app with display scaling.
  2. ☐ Increase screen brightness while leaving text size unchanged.
  3. ☐ Enlarge the webpage with browser zoom.

2. A community group is choosing a meeting time. The organizer needs your available times, not the details of your personal appointments. Which calendar information should you share?

  1. ☐ All calendar details through a public link.
  2. ☐ Free/busy times without appointment details.
  3. ☐ Full event descriptions with the organizer.

3. You begin typing a volunteer’s email address, and autocomplete selects another person with a similar name. The message is meant for only one volunteer. What should you do before sending?

  1. ☐ Check the full address and select the intended person.
  2. ☐ Accept the suggestion because you have emailed that person before.
  3. ☐ Send to both suggested addresses to reach the right person.

4. You need 15 copies of a library workshop flyer. You have changed the page layout since its last printout. Which step best avoids wasting paper?

  1. ☐ Print all 15 because the earlier layout printed correctly.
  2. ☐ Send the job twice so a spare set will be ready.
  3. ☐ Check the destination and preview, then inspect one printed copy.

5. You are ready to answer a community group question in your volunteer role. Your browser has opened your personal profile instead. What is the best next step?

  1. ☐ Copy the volunteer profile picture onto the personal reply.
  2. ☐ Select the volunteer profile and confirm who will see the reply.
  3. ☐ Reply from the personal profile because the same person owns both.

6. On Sunday evening, you finish a handout for a team that reviews work on weekdays. Feedback is needed by Thursday. Which message plan gives a clear, reasonable expectation?

  1. ☐ Send during the team’s usual hours and ask for feedback by Thursday.
  2. ☐ Send repeated reminders tonight until someone responds.
  3. ☐ Request a reply as soon as possible without stating the deadline.

7. A new volunteer asks you to explain a term in a shared handout. They have not used this program before. Which response respects their experience and supports learning?

  1. ☐ Tell them to memorize the term before asking more questions.
  2. ☐ Assume their age explains the question and complete the task for them.
  3. ☐ Define the term and connect it to the task they are trying to do.

8. You need to change a reminder in an updated calendar app. A saved tutorial shows a menu that is no longer there. How should you look for instructions that fit?

  1. ☐ Choose the oldest tutorial because it has collected the most views.
  2. ☐ Check current official help for the app and the reminder task.
  3. ☐ Follow the old tutorial’s button positions even when the labels differ.

9. You want an in-person computer class at a public library near Elkins, West Virginia. Which search is the best starting point for this task?

  1. ☐ Elkins WV public library computer classes
  2. ☐ computer classes around the world
  3. ☐ West Virginia places to visit

10. An online directory says a nearby community center offers free computer repairs. It does not say where that information came from. What should you do before relying on the offer?

  1. ☐ Assume the listing is verified because it is the first search result.
  2. ☐ Use the directory’s professional appearance as evidence that the offer is current.
  3. ☐ Confirm the service through the center’s official contact information.

11. You need a local workshop you can attend in person. A reliable community-center page lists a class in another state with no online option. What is missing for your task?

  1. ☐ A more recent date, which would make the distant class local.
  2. ☐ A class relevant to the place where you can attend.
  3. ☐ Proof that all information on the page must be false.

12. A fictional community-center form lets you request a workshop place. Some fields are optional and do not seem related to booking. What is the best approach before completing the form?

  1. ☐ Check the collector and purpose, then provide only information needed for the task.
  2. ☐ Treat every visible field as required and supply as much detail as possible.
  3. ☐ Assume a form with the center’s name can request any personal information.

13. Your community workshop guide has three main sections. You want clear structure that assistive technology can recognize, not just titles that look different. What should you use?

  1. ☐ Larger font sizes on ordinary paragraphs only.
  2. ☐ Pictures of the section titles instead of text headings.
  3. ☐ Built-in heading styles at the appropriate level.

14. Your practice supply list has 6 notebooks in C2, 4 folders in C3 and 3 pens in C4. You want the total quantity to update if any entry changes. Which formula should you use?

  1. ☐ =13
  2. ☐ =SUM(C2:C4)
  3. ☐ =C2+C4

15. You want to adapt a diagram from a website for a library workshop handout. You have not checked its license or permission terms. What should you do first?

  1. ☐ Check whether adapting it is allowed and what attribution is required.
  2. ☐ Change the colors and assume it is now entirely your own work.
  3. ☐ Use a screenshot because that avoids the need to check reuse terms.

16. A coauthor must correct the wording in your community resource guide before it is finished. Which sharing choice lets them make those changes directly?

  1. ☐ Send only a screenshot of the current guide.
  2. ☐ Share the editable file with reading access only.
  3. ☐ Share the editable file with suitable editing access.

17. You are emailing a community organizer to ask when the resource workshop starts. Which subject line most clearly describes your request?

  1. ☐ Resource workshop start time confirmed
  2. ☐ Please confirm the resource workshop start time
  3. ☐ Information about a community activity

18. You are emailing a library event reminder to several volunteers. They should receive the message without seeing the full list of other recipients’ addresses. Which field fits?

  1. ☐ Bcc for the addresses that should be hidden.
  2. ☐ Cc for the addresses that should be hidden.
  3. ☐ To for every address in the list.

19. A fictional video service offers a free month, and you only want to try one lesson. What information do you need before accepting the offer?

  1. ☐ Only the price shown for the first month.
  2. ☐ Only whether you can remove the app icon afterward.
  3. ☐ Renewal timing, the later price and cancellation terms.

20. A fictional free app offers an optional tool for a one-time $3 charge. You are reviewing the offer in class. What should happen before anyone authorizes that upgrade?

  1. ☐ Approve it now and review the cost after trying the tool.
  2. ☐ Review the separate charge and purchase terms before deciding.
  3. ☐ Accept it as free because downloading the app cost nothing.

21. A community team meets online and edits one shared workshop outline together during the meeting. What type of collaboration is this?

  1. ☐ Synchronous work because people are collaborating at the same time.
  2. ☐ Asynchronous work because the file is stored online.
  3. ☐ Separate-copy work because each person has a different computer.

22. You want a volunteer to point out unclear steps in a draft. They should add feedback without directly changing the document. The tool supports comments. Which permission should you give?

  1. ☐ View-only access to that volunteer.
  2. ☐ Editing access to anyone who receives the link.
  3. ☐ Commenter access to that volunteer.

23. A workshop guide uses an abbreviation without explaining it. New readers may not know what it means. Which comment would best help the author revise the guide?

  1. ☐ Make the abbreviation bold so an explanation is no longer needed.
  2. ☐ Spell out the abbreviation the first time so new readers can follow the steps.
  3. ☐ Improve this section because it needs more work.

24. You would like to save a video discussion from a library workshop for later study. The recording would include other participants. What should guide your next step?

  1. ☐ Follow the organizer’s recording rules and confirm the needed permission.
  2. ☐ Treat an invitation to attend as automatic permission to record.
  3. ☐ Record first because you intend to keep the file for your own use.

25. A volunteer cannot comfortably use a mouse, and another studies with the sound off. Which workshop page would let both people complete the task?

  1. ☐ One with keyboard access but no text alternative to spoken content.
  2. ☐ One with captions but buttons that work only with a mouse.
  3. ☐ One with keyboard access and accurate captions for spoken content.

26. In a fictional community forum, someone you have never met builds a friendly relationship and asks you to keep a request for private information secret. Their identity is unverified. What should you do?

  1. ☐ Accept a second profile sent by the same person as independent proof.
  2. ☐ Withhold the information and check their identity independently.
  3. ☐ Rely on the friendly conversation as proof of who they are.

27. An unfamiliar person leaves a USB drive labeled “useful workshop files” at the community computer lab. Staff have not approved it. What should you do?

  1. ☐ Keep it disconnected and ask authorized staff what to do.
  2. ☐ Open it briefly because the label describes useful material.
  3. ☐ Use a different lab computer to test whether the files open.

28. You are protecting a stored practice document. Preventing edits is not enough; someone without the key should be unable to read its contents. Which protection matches that need?

  1. ☐ Renaming the document to make it less obvious.
  2. ☐ Compressing it into an unencrypted ZIP file.
  3. ☐ Encrypting the stored data.
