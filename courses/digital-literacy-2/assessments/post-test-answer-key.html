One point per correct answer. Domain totals: four points each. Total: 28. Accept only the keyed choice for the knowledge score; use worksheet rubrics for demonstrated skills.

1. The text on a service website is too small. What should you try first?

C. Use the browser’s zoom control

Browser zoom changes webpage size. Choose the control that matches the problem.

Domain: Technology Basics · objective 1.1 · week 1

2. A volunteer scheduler only needs to know when you are unavailable. What should you share?

B. Free/busy information

Free/busy can support scheduling without disclosing event details. Verify the service’s options.

Domain: Technology Basics · objective 1.2 · week 1

3. Autocomplete suggests the wrong email address. What is the best response?

A. Check and choose the intended address

Automation can save time but needs review, especially for names, addresses and meaning.

Domain: Technology Basics · objective 1.3 · week 1

4. A handout is going to a shared lab printer. What should you verify first?

C. Printer destination, preview and a test page

Verify the destination and output before sending a large print job.

Domain: Technology Basics · objective 1.5 · week 1

5. Before posting in a professional group, what should you check?

B. The active identity, audience and account credentials

Check the identity and audience. Keep credentials distinct and use appropriate account protections.

Domain: Digital Citizenship · objective 2.1 · week 4

6. You need feedback next week. What is a considerate communication choice?

A. State the deadline and respect expected working hours

Timing and a clear response expectation help the recipient respond appropriately.

Domain: Digital Citizenship · objective 2.2 · week 4

7. A new teammate does not know an abbreviation. What should you do?

C. Explain it plainly without making assumptions

Inclusive communication explains context and avoids assumptions about age, culture or ability.

Domain: Digital Citizenship · objective 2.2 · week 4

8. You cannot find a command in an updated app. What is the best next step?

B. Use current official help and describe the exact task

Version-specific help and a precise question support independent learning.

Domain: Digital Citizenship · objective 2.3 · week 1

9. You need local public-library opening hours. Which query best fits?

A. Library name, town and opening hours

Search terms should identify the service and location needed for the task.

Domain: Information Management · objective 3.1 · week 2

10. A directory promises a local service without naming a source. What is the best check?

C. Confirm availability through the provider’s official contact

Responsible sources and corroborating evidence matter more than ranking or appearance.

Domain: Information Management · objective 3.2 · week 2

11. A reliable article explains an old program that no longer operates locally. What is the problem?

B. It may be outdated or irrelevant to this task

Accuracy, currency and relevance are separate questions.

Domain: Information Management · objective 3.2 · week 2

12. A service-request form asks for information unrelated to the request. What should you do?

A. Check the purpose and collector before providing it

Understand the collection purpose, provide only needed information, and check the result.

Domain: Information Management · objective 3.3 · week 2

13. How should you mark a document’s main sections?

C. Apply appropriate heading styles

Real heading styles provide structure; visual bolding alone does not.

Domain: Content Creation · objective 4.1 · week 3

14. C2, C3 and C4 contain quantities. Which formula totals that range?

B. =SUM(C2:C4)

A colon marks an inclusive cell range. Verify the formula by changing one input.

Domain: Content Creation · objective 4.1 · week 3

15. You want to adapt someone else’s diagram. What is the right first step?

A. Check allowed uses and attribution conditions

Find the actual reuse permission and follow its conditions.

Domain: Content Creation · objective 4.3 · week 3

16. A coauthor must change wording in your handout. What should you provide?

C. The editable file with suitable permissions

Choose the format and access that support the recipient’s task. PDF is often useful for finished layout.

Domain: Content Creation · objective 4.4 · week 3

17. Which subject best helps a recipient act on a schedule question?

B. Please confirm the resource workshop start time

A specific subject tells the recipient what the message concerns.

Domain: Communication · objective 5.1 · week 4

18. You use Bcc for an announcement. What protection does it provide?

A. Conceals that recipient list from other recipients

Bcc hides recipient addresses; it does not encrypt content or prevent forwarding.

Domain: Communication · objective 5.1 · week 4

19. A streaming offer starts at no charge. What should you check before accepting?

C. Whether it renews, the price and how to cancel

Free trials can become recurring subscriptions. Review the terms and total cost.

Domain: Communication · objective 5.2 · week 4

20. A downloaded app offers extra features for purchase. What is a sensible step?

B. Review the charge and purchase controls before authorizing

In-app purchases are separate transactions; check terms, controls and receipts.

Domain: Communication · objective 5.2 · week 4

21. A team coauthors a file during a live call. Which description fits?

A. Synchronous collaboration

Synchronous collaboration happens at the same time; asynchronous work happens at different times.

Domain: Collaboration · objective 6.1 · week 4

22. A partner only needs to leave feedback on your draft. Which permission is appropriate when available?

C. Commenter access

Commenter access fits feedback without granting direct editing rights.

Domain: Collaboration · objective 6.1 · week 4

23. Which comment best helps improve a shared handout?

B. Define this abbreviation so a new reader can follow the instructions.

Useful feedback names a specific change and explains the benefit.

Domain: Collaboration · objective 6.1 · week 4

24. You want to save a webinar discussion that includes participants. What should guide your decision?

A. Organizer rules and appropriate recording permission

Meeting etiquette includes checking recording permissions and respecting participants.

Domain: Collaboration · objective 6.2 · week 4

25. Which feature helps people use technology in different situations?

C. Captions and flexible input methods

Universal design increases usability across needs and situations.

Domain: Safety and Security · objective 7.1 · week 5

26. Someone invents a personal profile to manipulate a relationship. Which term fits?

B. Catfishing

Catfishing uses a false identity to build trust or manipulate someone. Pause and verify independently.

Domain: Safety and Security · objective 7.2 · week 5

27. A stranger offers a USB drive with a useful app. What is the safest class response?

A. Do not connect it; consult authorized support

Untrusted portable media can carry harmful software. Follow approved device procedures.

Domain: Safety and Security · objective 7.3 · week 5

28. An encrypted device is lost while powered off. What does encryption help protect?

C. The readability of stored data without its key

Encryption protects data readability. It does not replace other account, device or trust checks.
