✅ VA Login Setup Checklist

VUB Intermediate Computer Skills - Week 1

HANDOUT
Before you start: Setting up your VA.gov account gives you access to all VA online services. This process takes about 15-30 minutes. Have your information ready and follow these steps carefully.

📋 What You'll Need

  • A valid email address

    A personal email account that only you use; keep access to it for account confirmation and recovery

  • Your phone number

    For your chosen MFA method, such as text or a phone call; other methods are available

  • Government-issued photo ID

    Driver's license, state ID, or passport — you may need to take a photo of it

  • Your Social Security Number

    For identity verification purposes

  • A webcam or smartphone (optional)

    For video verification if needed — not always required

🚀 Step-by-Step Account Setup

  • 1

    Go to VA.gov

    Type va.gov directly into your browser's address bar. Don't use a search engine.

  • 2

    Click "Sign In"

    Look for the blue "Sign In" button in the top right corner of the page.

  • 3

    Choose Login.gov or ID.me

    Use your existing verified account, or choose one provider to create an account. Both are accepted for VA sign-in.

  • 4

    Enter your email and create a password

    Use a long, unique password that meets the selected provider's current requirements.

  • 5

    Verify your email

    Follow the confirmation instructions sent to your email. Return to the sign-in process you started.

  • 6

    Set up two-factor authentication

    Choose a supported method you can access, such as an authenticator app or a phone code. Keep recovery options secure.

  • 7

    Verify your identity

    Follow your provider's prompts to confirm your identity. Keep IDs and personal details off the classroom projector.

  • 8

    You're done!

    Once verified, you can access all VA online services with your new account.

🔐 Identity Verification Options

Your selected provider will guide you through its requirements. These options differ by provider:

📸 Online verification Follow the provider's prompts for accepted identity documents and any photo checks.
📹 Video Call Video chat with an ID.me representative who will verify your identity.
🏪 In-Person Login.gov offers participating post offices; ID.me offers participating UPS Store locations. Follow the provider's instructions before visiting.
📋 Need another option? Read the provider's help for your situation. Do not guess at verification answers or share codes with a helper.

⚠️ Common Problems & Solutions

Can't verify identity? Try the video call option — a real person can help.
Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password" on the login page to reset it.
Not receiving codes? Check your spam folder or try the phone call option instead of text.

🎉 Once You're Logged In, You Can:

  • Check your VA benefits status
  • View and track your disability claims
  • Open My HealtheVet on VA.gov for health services
  • Download official VA letters
  • Update your contact information
  • Apply for new benefits online
