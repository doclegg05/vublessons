The "Show" window: a live Windows 11 demo

This is a real Week 1 demo, making text bigger in Settings. It isn't a video or a screenshot. It's a working recreation of Windows 11 with a guided cursor. In class it waits for your clicker, one step per click. This preview plays by itself so you can watch it.

Camera zoomThe view pushes in on the exact button before the click, so the back row can see it, then pulls back out.
Spotlight + rippleEverything else dims, and a gold ring pulses where the cursor clicks.
Big captionsThe step in plain words, in large type beside the screen. It matches the worksheet line for line.
Your controlsBack, Next, Replay and ½ speed. Or switch to the real computer and repeat it live.
