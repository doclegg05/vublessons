Command Deck, with gorge moments

Teaching slides use the pure C command deck: grid floor, neon edges, nothing behind the text but dark space. B's dusk gorge appears only at the big moments: the week opener, the break, the week finale and the Week 6 launch. This keeps teaching slides as calm as possible and makes the beautiful scenes feel like an event.
