# Digital Literacy Level 2 course surface

Primary target: `courses/digital-literacy-2/index.html`.
Related targets: weekly presentations and resources, syllabus, instructor guide, assessments and `activities/resource-finder.html` within that course.

## Scope and visitor mode

An existing VUB brand extension for Operate/Read use: learners find the week's work, follow a lesson, complete a concrete task and preserve their results. Instructors use the same course tree to prepare and run six two-hour labs. This brief is deliberately stored in the course documentation boundary; it does not create global `.impeccable` metadata.

## Task and first view

The course home introduces Level 2 beside a six-week route map, then offers the syllabus, pre-test and first lesson. Weekly rows connect presentations, practice resources and media. A lesson starts with its title/objectives in the light panel; persistent desktop navigation shows the sequence and resources, while a compact toolbar and progress indicator establish location. Mobile learners reveal the same navigation with a menu button.

## Proof and memorable moment

Concrete lab tasks lead. Learners reveal a short explanation, answer a knowledge check and receive an immediate textual response, then use the worksheet to perform the task. In week 6 they can download and edit an actual fictional resource finder, demonstrate a visible change and test an empty result or keyboard path. This observable build-and-check loop is the culminating moment.

## Direction and authority

Retain the established VUB visual world and course controls. The light content panel and dark persistent sidebar support reading and orientation; practice surfaces distinguish doing from explanation. The reusable system is recorded in `DESIGN.md`; product constraints are recorded in `PRODUCT.md`.

No approved comp applies to this extension. There is no new-world selection, generated visual comp or platform-wide identity replacement to imply. The built artifact, user direction and repository brand rules are the authority.

## Constraints and open decisions

Cohort dates belong only to the syllabus. Preserve account-free practice, synthetic data, parallel classroom tests, explicit client-side storage behavior, accessible keyboard interaction, large lesson text, captions and transcripts. Printed assessment results are learner-controlled; no instructor submission is implied.

Live hosting or SaaS operation for learner-built apps is a separate future decision. The Explain Video Generator trial is a comparison, not a course dependency; delivered media uses the local production path documented in `HANDOFF.md`. This brief records the built surface and does not claim a live deployment or final review disposition; those remain in the delivery evidence.
