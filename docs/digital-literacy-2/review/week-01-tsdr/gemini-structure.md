# Week 1 structure and content review (Tell, Show, Do, Review + WIPPEA + adult learning)

## Verdict
The lesson contains excellent, highly relevant content and accurate simulations, but its current structure violates the core principle of chunking for adult learners. The single most important change is to break the massive 35-minute presentation block and 30-minute lab block into four short, distinct Tell-Show-Do-Review cycles so learners can practice each skill immediately after seeing it.

## WIPPEA map
| Stage | Where it happens | How well it meets purpose | Evidence | What to change |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Warm-up** | Lesson plan 0–10m; Slide 2 | Adequate | Asks learners to name a task they do well and one to make easier. | Tie it explicitly to the Level 1 course they just finished to activate prior technical knowledge. |
| **Introduction** | Lesson plan 0–10m; Slide 1 | Strong | Clear, plain-language objectives shown on screen and tied to real-world tasks. | None. |
| **Presentation** | Lesson plan 35–55m & 65–80m; Slides 4–19; Video 0:00–6:23 | Weak | 35 total minutes of continuous lecture/demonstration across 8+ distinct technical skills. | Break this massive block apart. Present one concept (e.g., Zoom), then immediately move to Practice. |
| **Practice** | Lesson plan 80–110m; Worksheet tasks 1–8 | Weak | A single 30-minute block at the end of the class. Learners will have forgotten the steps shown an hour earlier. | Interleave worksheet tasks immediately after their corresponding presentation slides. |
| **Evaluation** | Lesson plan 110–120m; Slides 20–21; Worksheet answer guide | Adequate | Knowledge checks on slides 20-21; instructor checks worksheet. | Move the knowledge checks to the end of their specific skill cycles (e.g., Slide 20 after the Sound cycle). |
| **Application** | Lesson plan 110–120m; Slides 23–24; Video 6:24 | Strong | Asks learners to name one thing they can now do and a next practice step; emphasizes restoring lab settings. | None. |

**Overall flow:** The current order is a traditional, outdated "lecture then lab" model. It fails the cyclical WIPPEA requirement. Presenting page zoom, display scaling, sound, connections, printing, calendars, and automation in one giant block will cause severe cognitive overload for older learners. Furthermore, the lesson plan's instruction to "Play the explainer" at minute 65 contradicts the instruction to "Use relevant chapters during the demonstration phases." The video chapters must be split up and used as the "Show" step in individual skill cycles.

## Does the content make sense for this audience?
Yes, the tone is respectful and the analogies (radio/headphones for sound routing; workbench/filing cabinet for memory/storage) are brilliant for older adults. However, the pacing is entirely unrealistic. Expecting older learners with potential vision, hearing, or dexterity challenges to remember 8 different technical processes demonstrated over 35 minutes, and then execute them flawlessly in a 30-minute block, will result in failure and frustration. The fictional data (library, Room A) is safe and low-risk, which is excellent for this demographic.

## Recommended restructure
This revised 2-hour running order uses short TSDR cycles, keeps the pre-test, and uses all existing materials.

*   **0–10m: Warm-up & Intro.** Welcome, goals. Slides 1–2. Worksheet #1.
*   **10–35m: Pre-test.** Slide 3. Quiet work.
*   **35–50m: Cycle 1 (Vision & Sound).** 
    *   *Tell/Show:* Slides 4–7. Play Video Ch 1–4. 
    *   *Do:* Worksheet #2 & #3. 
    *   *Review:* Slide 20 (Knowledge check).
*   **50–65m: Cycle 2 (Hardware & Printing).** 
    *   *Tell/Show:* Slides 9–12. Play Video Ch 5–6. 
    *   *Do:* Worksheet #4 & #7. 
    *   *Review:* Instructor checks screens.
*   **65–75m: Break.** Screen-free pause.
*   **75–95m: Cycle 3 (Calendars).** 
    *   *Tell/Show:* Slides 13–15. Play Video Ch 7–8. 
    *   *Do:* Worksheet #5 & #6. 
    *   *Review:* Slide 21 (Knowledge check).
*   **95–110m: Cycle 4 (Automation & Help).** 
    *   *Tell/Show:* Slides 16, 19. Play Video Ch 9. 
    *   *Do:* Worksheet #8. 
    *   *Review:* Partner feedback check.
*   **110–120m: Application & Wrap-up.** Slides 22–24. Play Video Ch 10. Ensure lab settings are restored.
*(Note: Slides 8 and 17 are omitted from the core flow to save time, as they have no worksheet activities. Use them only for fast finishers).*

## Top 10 changes, ranked
1.  **Restructure into 4 short TSDR cycles:** (Lesson Plan). Move from a "lecture-then-lab" format to interleaved Tell-Show-Do-Review cycles (see Recommended Restructure). *Why: WIPPEA/Andragogy (chunking, cognitive load).* **Effort: Medium.**
2.  **Split video usage by chapter:** (Lesson Plan). Do not play the video in one block at minute 65. Play the relevant chapter during the "Show" phase of each specific cycle. *Why: TSDR (Show must immediately precede Do).* **Effort: Small.**
3.  **Move worksheet tasks to immediately follow instruction:** (Lesson Plan/Worksheet). Learners should complete Worksheet #2 right after Slide 6, not 45 minutes later. *Why: TSDR (Immediate practice).* **Effort: Small.**
4.  **Integrate Knowledge Checks into cycles:** (Slides 20-21). Move Slide 20 to follow the Sound cycle, and Slide 21 to follow the Calendar cycle. *Why: TSDR (Immediate review).* **Effort: Small.**
5.  **Update printing default context:** (Slide 11/Instructor notes). Mention that Windows 11 often manages the default printer based on the last used device. *Why: Content correctness (2026 OS behavior).* **Effort: Small.**
6.  **Make Slides 8 and 17 optional:** (Lesson Plan). "Browser choices" and "Cloud services" have no corresponding practice tasks and clutter the lesson. *Why: Andragogy (Orientation/Relevance).* **Effort: Small.**
7.  **Rewrite Lesson Plan Objectives:** (Lesson Plan). Update the bulleted list to strict ABCD format so the instructor knows exactly what behavior to evaluate. *Why: WIPPEA (Backward design).* **Effort: Small.**
8.  **Clarify Display Scaling limits:** (Slide 5/Instructor notes). Explicitly tell learners they will *not* practice Display Scaling today to protect the shared lab environment. *Why: Andragogy (Safe practice).* **Effort: Small.**
9.  **Integrate partner switching into the cycles:** (Lesson Plan). Instead of switching driver/coach halfway through a 30-minute lab, have them switch roles between Cycle 1 and Cycle 2. *Why: WIPPEA (Varied practice).* **Effort: Small.**
10. **Tie Warm-up to Level 1:** (Lesson Plan). Explicitly ask learners to recall one skill they mastered in Level 1 to build confidence. *Why: WIPPEA (Activate prior knowledge).* **Effort: Small.**
