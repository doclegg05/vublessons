# Week 3 structure and content review (Tell, Show, Do, Review + WIPPEA + adult learning)

## Verdict
The lesson contains highly relevant, practical, and accurate content for 2026, but its structure completely fails the cognitive needs of older adult learners by grouping instruction into massive 20-minute "watch and listen" blocks. The single most important change is to abandon the block-teaching structure and break the lesson into short, distinct Tell-Show-Do-Review micro-cycles for each application (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Export) so learners can practice immediately after seeing a demonstration.

## WIPPEA map

| Stage | Where it happens | How well it meets purpose | Evidence | What to change |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Warm-up** | LP 0–10 min | Adequate | "Review last week’s source trail and folder choices." | Add a specific hook connecting last week's folders to *today's* task of saving a multi-file resource pack. |
| **Introduction** | LP 10–30 min (start); Slides 1–2; Video Ch 1 | Strong | Scenario of a neighbor looking for computer help; clear objectives on Slide 1. | Keep as is, but ensure it happens immediately after the pre-test. |
| **Presentation** | LP 10–30 min & 60–80 min; Slides 3–18; Video Ch 2–9 | Weak | Two massive 20-minute blocks covering multiple distinct software tools (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Video). | Break into four short presentations (Docs, Sheets, Slides/Media, Export) immediately followed by their respective practice. |
| **Practice** | LP 30–50 min & 80–110 min; Worksheet tasks 1–8 | Weak | Delayed up to 20 minutes after the presentation. Learners will forget the first steps shown. | Move each worksheet task to immediately follow its specific Tell/Show segment. |
| **Evaluation** | LP 110–120 min; Slides 19–20; Answer Guide | Adequate | Peer review for readability/accuracy; Knowledge checks on Slides 19–20. | Move Slides 19 and 20 to immediately follow their respective topics (Formulas and Track Changes) rather than lumping them at the end. |
| **Application** | Slide 23; Video Ch 10 | Strong | "Carry the same routine into a household budget, a community notice..." | Emphasize this transfer during the final wrap-up. |

**Overall flow:** The current order violates the cyclical nature of WIPPEA and Tell-Show-Do-Review. By teaching Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Video editing in large blocks before allowing practice, the cognitive load is far too high for Level 2 older adults. The video chapters are currently meant to be played during the long demonstration phases; they should instead be used as the "Show" component of short, individual skill cycles.

For older veterans, this guarantees cognitive overload, frustration, and a high failure rate during the lab, as they will forget the Word steps by the time the Excel demonstration finishes. It must be restructured into short cycles.

## Does the content make sense for this audience?
The **pacing is highly unrealistic**. Teaching Word (formatting, track changes), Excel (formulas, charts), PowerPoint (slide design), Image Editing (cropping), Video Editing (trimming), and File Management (Exporting PDF/CSV) in a single 2-hour session is a massive amount of context switching. Even with fictional data, jumping between 4 to 5 different applications is too heavy for Level 2 older adults. 

**Logistical leap:** The lesson plan says "partners suggest and review one edit" (Track Changes). On individual lab workstations, they cannot easily track changes on each other's documents unless they use cloud sharing (taught in Week 2, but time-consuming) or physically swap seats. The LP mentions "Pair a driver and coach, then switch," which implies seat-swapping. This must be explicitly managed by the instructor to avoid confusion.

## Recommended restructure
*This revised 120-minute running order incorporates the requested 25-minute pre-test, breaks the lesson into short Tell-Show-Do-Review cycles, and makes video editing an optional/demo-only topic to protect pacing.*

| Minutes | Stage | Action (Using existing materials) |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **0–25** | Evaluation | **Pre-test** (25 mins). |
| **25–35** | Warm-up & Intro | **Retrieve & Hook:** Review Week 2 folders. Show Slides 1–2. Play Video Ch 1. Establish the "neighbor help" scenario. |
| **35–55** | Cycle 1: Docs | **Tell/Show:** Slides 3–7. Play Video Ch 2 & 3. Demo headings and track changes.<br>**Do:** Learners complete WS Tasks 1, 2, 3, 4.<br>**Review:** Slide 20 (Knowledge check moved here). Driver/coach swap seats to accept/reject edits. |
| **55–65** | Break | Posture and screen break (10 mins). |
| **65–85** | Cycle 2: Sheets | **Tell/Show:** Slides 8–11. Play Video Ch 4, 5, 6. Demo SUM formula and basic chart.<br>**Do:** Learners complete WS Task 5 and the chart portion of Task 6.<br>**Review:** Slide 19 (Knowledge check moved here). Verify the total changed to 28. |
| **85–100** | Cycle 3: Slides | **Tell/Show:** Slides 12–13, 16. Play Video Ch 7. Demo 3-slide structure and image cropping. *(Make Video editing/Slide 14/Video Ch 8 instructor-demo only to save time).*<br>**Do:** Learners complete WS Task 6 (slides) and Task 7 (crop image).<br>**Review:** Check partner's slides for readability. |
| **100–115** | Cycle 4: Export | **Tell/Show:** Slides 17–18. Play Video Ch 9. Demo exporting to PDF and CSV.<br>**Do:** Learners complete WS Task 8.<br>**Review:** Open the PDF to prove it worked. |
| **115–120** | Application | **Wrap-up:** Slide 23 and Video Ch 10. Discuss how to use this for a household budget. |

## Top 10 changes, ranked

1. **Break up the presentation blocks (WIPPEA/Andragogy):** Divide the two 20-minute Tell/Show blocks into four distinct micro-cycles (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Export) so learners practice immediately after watching. *(Medium effort)*
2. **Add the 25-minute pre-test to the timeline (Evaluation):** Adjust the lesson plan minutes to accommodate this required assessment at the very beginning of the class. *(Small effort)*
3. **Move Knowledge Checks (Evaluation):** Relocate Slide 19 (Formulas) to follow Cycle 2, and Slide 20 (Track Changes) to follow Cycle 1, rather than keeping them at the end of the deck. *(Small effort)*
4. **Make Video Editing a Demo Only (Cognitive Load):** Older adults learning Word, Excel, and PPT in one day do not have the cognitive bandwidth to also learn a video editor (Slide 14, WS 7). Treat it as an instructor demonstration to explain the concepts of trim/split. *(Small effort)*
5. **Clarify the Track Changes logistics (Practice):** Explicitly instruct learners to physically swap seats ("driver and coach") for WS Task 4 so they can accept/reject a partner's edit without needing to set up cloud sharing. *(Small effort)*
6. **Integrate Video Chapters into Cycles (Show):** Instead of playing the video chapters all at once during the long presentation phases, play the specific 1-minute chapter right before the learners do that specific worksheet task. *(Small effort)*
7. **Separate Chart Practice from Slide Practice (Do):** Have learners create the chart (Slide 11) while they are already in the spreadsheet (WS 5), rather than waiting until they are building presentation slides (WS 6). *(Small effort)*
8. **Define "Alternative Text" explicitly (Tell):** Slide 7 mentions "useful alternative text," but novices may not know where to click to add it. Ensure the instructor demo explicitly shows right-clicking an image to add Alt Text. *(Small effort)*
9. **Connect Warm-up to Today's Goal (Warm-up):** When reviewing Week 2's folder choices (LP 0-10), explicitly tell learners they will use those exact folder skills today to save their multi-file resource pack. *(Small effort)*
10. **Remove Mac references if inapplicable (Context):** If the lab is strictly Windows 11, remove or verbally gloss over the "Command" key references on Slide 4 to prevent novice confusion. *(Small effort)*
