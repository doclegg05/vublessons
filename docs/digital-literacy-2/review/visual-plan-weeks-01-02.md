## Direction and implementation boundaries

Retain the approved navy/gold learning-app interface, large readable text, semantic controls, existing practice feedback, and curriculum. Use believable West Virginia home and community photographs to establish a familiar situation, and authored diagrams or fictional interfaces to teach a precise operation. Do not place meaningful instructions inside generated photographs. Every diagram needs text labels as well as visual cues. No automatic slide advancement, drag-only tasks, live submissions, or implied changes to operating-system settings.

The fourth Nano Banana Pro workstation candidate is the preferred week-1 scenario image, pending full-resolution artifact and crop review. The third safety candidate belongs primarily to week 5; do not spread it across unrelated weeks just because it looks good. The current video composition repeats one weekly illustration in a narrow 400px photographic column, or 270px wide-layout column: replacing the file alone will not make all chapters visually specific. Chapter-level art selection and full-stage authored demos are required.
