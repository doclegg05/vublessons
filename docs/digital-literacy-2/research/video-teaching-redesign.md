# Deeper video teaching design

Audience: adult veterans who bring varied practical experience and technology familiarity. Do not infer ability from age. Ask learners to choose an example from their own routine; all recorded examples use fictional information.

## Evidence and design decisions

- [CDC adult-learning training guide](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/about/develop-training-captivating-and-motivating-adult-learners.html): relevance, active participation, practice and feedback. Each lesson establishes a useful task, invites prior experience, models a decision, provides guided practice and ends with transfer. These are design choices informed by the guide, not a claim that longer videos alone improve learning.
- [W3C older users and accessibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/older-users/): use accessible controls, legible visuals, captions and learner control. Videos are divided into named chapters; replay/seek and transcripts support different paces. Do not equate older age with cognitive impairment.
- [Microsoft text and app size](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Accessibility/windows/make-text-and-apps-bigger): distinguish changing one page from system-wide text/display changes.
- [Microsoft OneDrive recovery](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onedrive/restore-deleted-files-or-folders-in-onedrive) and [Google Drive sharing](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822?hl=en): distinguish storage, sync, recovery and task-appropriate permissions; menu names depend on the service.
- [Microsoft accessible Word documents](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/word/make-your-word-documents-accessible-to-people-with-disabilities) and [SUM](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/functions/sum-function): semantic structure, meaningful links and cell-range formulas support usable outputs.
- [NIST phishing guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber/guidance-topic/phishing): independently verify suspicious requests.
- [MDN learning pathway](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development): distinguish HTML, CSS and JavaScript and test observable behavior. The course extension is a local fictional prototype, not a secure production SaaS service.

## Teaching pattern

1. Connect to a task the learner values, without assuming prior software knowledge.
2. Explain the concept through a familiar physical-world analogy, then state its limits.
3. Demonstrate the action and narrate the reason for each choice.
4. Show a plausible mistake, its visible consequence and a recovery route.
5. Ask for a prediction or a paused task; then explain the answer.
6. Ask learners to transfer the routine to a different example and describe their evidence.

Target approximately 6–8 minutes of instruction per week, in about ten replayable chapters, with optional pauses outside the recorded runtime. Fit viewing and practice into the existing 120-minute plan, not on top of it. Reuse familiar browser, file, email and document tasks; offer a modeled path when an app or account is unavailable. Keep personal records and real account credentials out of demonstrations.
