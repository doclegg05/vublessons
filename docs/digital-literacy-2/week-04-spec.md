## Facts to keep the same everywhere

- Feedback example: "In step 2, add where the desk is: Room A, first floor, so first-time visitors can find it." The old video used "add the contact number after the steps", which is close to post-test item 16; the old answer key said opening hours.
- Offers: A is free for 30 days, then $6 a month until you cancel; B is $60 a year, paid now. A costs $12 for 3 months and $66 for a year. The trial-date round uses March 3, ending April 2, so cancel by April 1; course pages carry no cohort dates. The old deck had $5 a month against $48 a year with no trial, and the old video had a trial at $12 a month.
- Zelle is inside bank apps now (the standalone app closed in 2025). The account check reads the email's From line, not the Edge profile picture. Phones: require a password or authentication for every purchase.
- Rounds avoid the wording of test items 13 to 17.

## Lesson

Source: `scripts/dl2/mission-control/week4.py`. 33 slides, no break, no timer, no capstone. Minutes: video with pauses 30, welcome and overview 5, Mission 4A 20, 4B 18, 4C 18, 4D 14, individual check 8, close 7.

- 4A Send a clear request (worksheet email drafts): rounds Make it actionable; Say it so anyone can follow.
- 4B Improve one shared copy (Word comments by swapping seats): rounds Vague to useful; Live or later?
- 4C Join with care (Mission 4C practice page, the existing meeting simulation): rounds Hear and be heard (Windows Sound settings, set the input back afterwards); Meeting or webinar?; Is the post right? (a fictional pantry post against the pantry's page, as Week 2 checks an AI answer).
- 4D Check before paying (worksheet offers and Calculator): rounds First-year cost; What is missing?; Inspect a checkout (Mission 4D practice page, the existing checkout simulation).
